# Ticket T-057b: World visuals for M3 - massive maps (instancing), small gifts + golden phase, collapse, pads, ward
Target: INKLING (max effort)

## Context
M3 (design doc below; section 0 amendments are binding) brings MASSIVE layered maps (hundreds of platforms,
pillars, stairs, jump pads), ~50-400 small gifts, and a two-phase level: collect every gift, then the map COLLAPSES
from the outside in while every collected gift's spot becomes a golden gift (currency) and the beacon on the spawn
hub ignites. Same visual identity as now (checkerboard tops, glowing edges, themed colours, misty void, bloom).
Human references: huge walkway networks with stacked layers on dark pillars, hanging vines and red glowing
lantern-like lights as decoration, many small dark-glowing gifts lining the walkways.

Rules: React Three Fiber (Next 16, React 19, TS 6 strict, three 0.187, drei). No `any`; no non-null assertions
outside tests; named exports; "use client"; no per-frame allocation; mutate only `ref.current` (react-hooks v7
immutability rule); pure logic in `.ts` files with vitest tests; complete files.

## Task
1. Platforms at scale: render with `InstancedMesh` grouped by kind (NORMAL, CRUMBLE, JUMP_PAD), keeping the
   checkerboard tops (a shader/texture in world space so instances tile seamlessly) and glowing edges; tall thin
   boxes (pillars: height > 3 x their larger horizontal size) get a dark stone material without checker. Tile states
   from `store.tiles`: TILE_WARN shakes + flashes amber->red; TILE_GONE sinks/fades over 1.2 s then hides (per-
   instance matrix/colour updates, no geometry rebuild); TILE_FRETTED shows dark-green crawling veins. JUMP_PAD tops
   glow with a pulsing ring and an upward chevron. Must stay smooth with 600 platforms.
2. Gifts: small (0.34 u) instanced gifts that bob; phase 1 shows untaken normal gifts. In phase 2 (`store.levelPhase`
   != LEVEL_COLLECT) every COLLECTED normal gift position shows a golden gift unless its id is in
   `store.goldenTaken` or its platform is TILE_GONE (find the platform under the gift once per level). Golden gifts
   are bright gold with a sparkle. Remove the old separate golden-gift logic from GameClient.
3. Beacon on the spawn hub: dormant grey in phase 1, ignites (tall light pillar) in phase 2.
4. HUD: phase-2 banner "THE VOID IS COLLAPSING - grab the gold, get back to the beacon" + a collapse countdown from
   `store.collapseMs`; the gift counter shows "x / total" in phase 1 and golden collected in phase 2.
5. Bulwark ward: a translucent hexagonal dome around a player whose `wardFrames > 0` (local from the predicted
   controller state; remote players: skip). Delete the blink flash (the blinker class is gone) - remove its use.
6. Decoration (no collision): procedurally place hanging vines under some walkway edges and small red glowing
   lantern lights on posts at some platform corners (instanced, deterministic from the level seed, density capped,
   max ~1 lantern light per 40 u of walkway and NO real lights - emissive + bloom only).
7. Wire it all in `GameClient.tsx`; keep EnemyField usage unchanged (another ticket rewrites EnemyField).
