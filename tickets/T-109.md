# T-109 — Visual pass: glider model + gift colours

**Agent:** model:opencode/opencode/inkling-2-small-free (parallel instance, max effort)
**Owns:** glider model, gift/glow rendering, related HUD visuals.

## 1. Glider model
A real, readable procedural glider through the existing pipeline: distinct flight
silhouette, readable against fog, no new deps, no binary assets. Capture
screenshots with `just capture`.

## 2. Gift colours
- **Normal gifts become BLACK**, keeping their glow: a black body with a visible
  glow, not a black blob that disappears against the floor.
- **Golden gifts are GOLD**, visually distinct from normal at gameplay distance.
- Update every place colour matters: models, glow tint, radar/HUD arrows, UI
  references.

## 3. LURE coherence (JUDGMENT CALL 2 — hard rule)
LURE's glimmer must mimic the **new BLACK normal-gift glow only**. It must never
read as a golden gift, and it must not be confusable with T-112's straggler
highlight. Verify after the palette change and adjust LURE's disguise if the
highlight effect could be mistaken for it.

Current LURE implementation: `sim/src/rules/enemies/lure.rs` plus the client
renderer. Its whole threat is being mistaken for a gift, so a palette change that
makes LURE *more* obvious is a gameplay regression even though it is a visual
improvement.

## Done when
Screenshots show the glider model and the gift palette; LURE's disguise still
works; vitest updated; gates green.

## Autonomous choices
- Black normal gifts keep a glow rather than going matte: a matte black gift on a
  dark floor is invisible, which would make the game harder in a way the owner
  did not ask for.
- LURE keeps its existing silhouette and fog behaviour; only its glimmer tint
  follows the new normal-gift colour.
