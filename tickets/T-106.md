# T-106 — Ramp fixes: orientation, texture, movement

**Agent:** model:opencode/opencode/inkling-2-small-free (max effort)
**Owns:** ramp asset, slope collision, ramp orientation code.
**References:** PANEL 1 + PANEL 2 (black ramps, red section, misaligned against
the checkered floor, X marks the problem spots).

## Root cause found before dispatch (orientation + movement)

`levelgen.rs::build_geometry` rotated a pattern's AABB by `turns` but passed
`p.kind` through **unchanged**:

```rust
(
    p.kind,   // <-- not remapped
    translate_aabb(&rotate_aabb(&Aabb { min: p.min, max: p.max }, turns), offset),
)
```

`rotate_xz` maps `(x, z) -> (z, -x)`, so one quarter-turn sends +X to -Z, -Z to
-X, -X to +Z and +Z to +X. A `RAMP_PZ` placed at 90 degrees therefore had its
geometry running along X while `world::surface_at` still computed the slope
along Z. The collision surface and the visible wedge disagreed: the player walks
on a slope that is not drawn, and the drawn ramp appears to face the wrong way.
Roughly three quarters of the 141,243 placed ramps were affected.

**Fixed:** `rotate_ramp_kind(kind, turns)` with the cycle
`PX -> NZ -> NX -> PZ -> PX`, applied in `build_geometry`. Two regression tests
in `levelgen.rs`:
- `ramp_kinds_rotate_with_their_geometry` — the cycle, the 4-turn identity, that
  a single turn always changes the axis, and that non-ramp kinds are untouched.
- `rotated_ramp_surface_rises_along_the_rotated_kind` — for all four turns, the
  surface sampled at the kind's claimed high end is strictly higher than at the
  low end.

## Remaining work in this ticket

### 1. Texture fit (not yet fixed)
The surface shader checkers in **world space**: `vec2 fp = vWorldPos.xz * 2.6`
(`platformShaders.ts:113`). The `uv` attribute built in `createWedgeGeometry`
(`Platforms.tsx:132-134`) is dead code — the shader never reads it, and its V
coordinate is a per-vertex `abs(v[1]+0.5) > abs(v[2]+0.5)` heuristic that is
discontinuous across a face.

Because the checker is projected straight down, cells on an incline are stretched
by `1/cos(theta)` along the slope. On a 0.5-slope ramp that is a 1.12x stretch,
which is exactly the "texture does not fit" read against the flat floor next to
it. `vWorldPos.y` also feeds the band term (`platformShaders.ts:135`), so a ramp
picks up banding a flat platform does not.

Fix: give the ramp instance a slope descriptor (axis + rise + run) and, for ramp
instances only, compute the checker coordinate from **arc length along the
incline** rather than raw XZ, so cells stay square on the slope and line up with
the flat pieces at both ends. Delete the dead `uv` attribute rather than
repairing it.

### 2. Movement regression tests (not yet written)
The orientation fix removes the largest cause, but the ticket still needs the
seam/edge coverage the brief asks for, at the slope-collision layer:
- walking up, down and across a ramp at full speed
- standing still on a ramp (no creep, no jitter)
- landing on a ramp from a jump
- ramp-to-platform seam at the low edge and at the high edge
- ramp-to-ramp joint (two ramps meeting at a shared edge)
- stepping off the side of a ramp

## Autonomous choices

  renderer: the kind is the contract between sim and client, so it must describe
  the geometry it is attached to.

  prediction agree by construction, per the standing rule.
