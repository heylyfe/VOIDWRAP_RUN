# T-129 — Hazard pool: three patterns were carrying every trampoline

**Cycle:** 13 · **Agent:** orchestrator · **Status:** PASS

## Why

The Cycle 12 log flagged hazard coverage as thin: crumble 7 patterns, tripmine 4,
trampoline 3, out of 135. The concern was that a 20-level run might never see a
trampoline.

## Measurement — the concern was wrong, the diagnosis was right

Level coverage was already healthy, because the few patterns were placed very often:

| hazard | levels containing it | patterns |
|---|---|---|
| `hazard:trampoline` | 3,256 / 6,000 (54.3%) | **3** |
| `hazard:tripmine` | 3,246 / 6,000 (54.1%) | **4** |
| `hazard:crumble` | 2,916 / 6,000 (48.6%) | 7 |
| `hazard:fog` | 2,525 / 6,000 (42.1%) | 7 |

So players do meet trampolines — but **every trampoline in the game came from one of
three shapes**. That is a variety defect, not a coverage defect, and it is the kind
of thing an aggregate rate hides.
## Change — additive only

Five new patterns, no existing entry touched:

| id | file | hazard | shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pa2_ember_bounce` | patterns-a | trampoline | two trampolines stacked, 3 u rise each, 18 u long |
| `pb_frost_bounce` | patterns-b | trampoline | trampoline in a 4 u pit, bounces back to the deck |
| `pb_void_bounce` | patterns-b | trampoline | two trampolines bridging a void span, 19 u |
| `pa2_mine_gauntlet` | patterns-a | tripmine | three mine strips with safe islands between, 20 u |
| `pa3_mine_switchback` | patterns-a | tripmine | two mine landings on a 2 u-step switchback, 19 u |

All five are inside the `[10, 20] u` reference band, carry no ramps (so no slope-cap
interaction), and keep every height delta at 2-4 u.

## A silent-eligibility bug found on the way

`pa3_mine_switchback` was placed **0 times over 6,000 levels**. Cause:
`pattern.rs::allowed_after` rejects `mech:fork` on anything that is not a junction:

```rust
if candidate.has_mechanic(MechanicTag::Fork) && candidate.role != ChunkRole::Junction {
    return false;
}
```

The piece has two sockets, so it is a path, and the tag made it permanently
ineligible. Retagged `mech:climb` (it has two 2 u steps). It now places 958 times.

This is the third instance of the same failure mode in this project — a piece that
parses, validates, and is counted by `validate-content`, but never reaches a level
(`platform_kind_from_str` missing `"TRIPMINE"`, `verify_socket` rejecting ramps, and
now this). The `every_shipped_chunk_parses_strictly` count assertion catches the
first two; it does not catch an eligibility filter. Logged as a candidate for a
future cycle: a harness assertion that every catalog pattern is placed at least once
over N seeds.

## Tuning notes

- Trampoline share of hazard-tagged placements rose 20.5% -> 29.1% because the new
  pieces are longer and carry more trampoline platforms each. If trampolines start
  feeling common, drop `pb_void_bounce`'s `min_level` from 12 to 14 rather than
  removing a pattern.
- `pa2_mine_gauntlet` has three mine strips in 20 u — the densest tripmine piece in
  the catalog. It is `difficulty: 3`, `min_level: 7`. Watch it in Extreme/Chaos.
- `pa3_mine_switchback`'s 2 u steps are climbable but not ramped; if the owner wants
  every vertical transition ramped, this piece needs a redesign, not a tweak.

## Choice log

- **Add patterns rather than raise placement weight.** Weighting the three existing
  trampoline pieces harder would have made the repetition worse, not better.
- **No ramps in the new pieces.** Ramps are the one geometry class with a hard slope
  cap and a socket-anchor rule; keeping the new pieces ramp-free means they cannot
  regress `5.ramps` or `5.ramp-anchors`.
- **Retag rather than change role.** Making `pa3_mine_switchback` a junction would
  have required a third socket, which changes the piece's shape. The tag was the
  thing that was wrong.
