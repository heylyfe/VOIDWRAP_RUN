# T-105 — 12 level-1 patterns (min_level 1 only)

**Agent:** `model:opencode/opencode/inkling-2-small-free`, max effort.
**Scope:** author pattern JSON only. Do not touch any file in the repo.

## Why

`4.near-duplicates` is now **0.35%** (threshold 0.2%) after T-104, down from 14.93%.
**Every one of the 11 remaining flagged pairs is at level 1.** A level-1 plan is 13–17
slots long and only **25** path/junction patterns are eligible at level 1, so two level-1
plans collide. Widening the level-1 pool is the only lever left.

## What to produce

**12 new patterns**, all with `min_level` **exactly 1**, ids `pa4_<slug>`:

- **9 `path`** — exactly 2 sockets.
- **3 `junction`** — 3 or 4 sockets, every socket `width >= 6`.

Spread `difficulty` across 1, 2 and 3. Vary shape, height and mechanic mix — do not
produce 12 variations of one bridge. Prefer compact footprints (extent 12–16) so they
stitch easily at level 1.

## Hard rules (a violation rejects the pattern)

1. `id` matches `^pa4_[a-z0-9_]+$`; `name` is Title Case, plain words.
2. `role` is `path` or `junction`.
3. `min_level` is exactly **1**. `difficulty` 1..3.
4. `tags` carries **1–2** `theme:` tags and **1–3** `mech:` tags.
   - themes: `theme:ember`, `theme:void`, `theme:frost`, `theme:ruin`, `theme:garden`,
     `theme:machine`, `theme:spire`, `theme:hollow`
   - mechs: `mech:climb`, `mech:drop`, `mech:span`, `mech:bounce`, `mech:crawl`,
     `mech:gauntlet`, `mech:rest`, `mech:loot`, `mech:fork`, `mech:dead_end`
   - Extra free-form tags (e.g. `"bridge"`) are legal and ignored.
5. **Footprint extent** `max(maxX-minX, maxZ-minZ)` must be in **[10, 20]**. Aim 12–16.
6. **Sockets** sit on the pattern's own bounding edge for their `facing`
   (`N` = max Z, `S` = min Z, `E` = max X, `W` = min X), and `pos.y` must equal a
   platform's `max.y` exactly, with `pos.x`/`pos.z` inside that platform's footprint.
   Socket `width` 3..8 (junctions >= 6).
7. **Ramps** (`RAMP_PX`, `RAMP_NX`, `RAMP_PZ`, `RAMP_NZ`): run >= 2, width >= 3,
   `rise / run <= 0.5`. `RAMP_PX` rises along +X, `RAMP_NX` along -X, `RAMP_PZ` along +Z,
   `RAMP_NZ` along -Z. A ramp's `min.y` is its low edge and `max.y` its high edge.
8. **Stacking clearance**: two platforms whose XZ footprints overlap must be at least
   **4.0 u** apart vertically, or exactly flush (no overlap in Y at all).
9. **Adjacent top deltas**: where two platforms touch along an edge, their top heights
   must be equal (~0) or differ by **1.0–6.0 u**. A 0.5 u half-step is a violation.
10. **Gift slots** sit on a platform top: `pos.y` = platform `max.y + 0.3`, and
    `pos.x`/`pos.z` inside the footprint. `elevated: true` slots may float up to 0.8
    above the top. **Altar slots** are `[x, y, z]` with `y` exactly a platform top.
11. Every platform must be reachable from every socket's platform by walking, a
    <= 0.55 u step, a jump of at most 6 u horizontal for a flat rise, or a ramp. Do not
    add tall pillars with no ramp — an unreachable platform is a latent generator bug.
12. `enemy_slots` is `[]`; `beacon` is `null`.

## Output contract

For each pattern emit exactly:

```
=== NEW PATTERN ===
{ ...one complete JSON object, pretty-printed... }
```

Nothing else between blocks. No commentary inside a block. 12 blocks total.
