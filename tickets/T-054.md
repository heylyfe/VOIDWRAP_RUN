# Ticket T-054: The 25-chunk map catalog (data) + catalog validator
Target: INKLING (max effort)

## Context
M3 replaces random flat roads with MASSIVE maps assembled from hand-designed chunks (design doc below; section 0
amendments are binding: huge looping networks, stacked layers, walkable 0.5 u stairs, wide paths, lots of gift
slots lining the walkways). Another engineer writes the Rust assembler against the format spec; you author the
catalog data and a validator. The human's reference: huge interconnected walkway networks, loops and grids with many
junctions, several stacked layers on pillars joined by stairs, towers above everything.

## Task
1. `design/chunks.json`: the 25 chunks of catalog 4.2 (ids `ch01_...` to `ch25_...`), following CHUNK_FORMAT.md
   exactly, with the amendments: stairs use 0.5 u risers; walkways >= 3 u (most 4 u), hubs >= 6 u; add pillar boxes
   under elevated walkways for the layered look; generous gift slots along every walkway (every 2.0-2.6 u: a 12 u
   walkway gets ~5 slots); 2-4 sockets on hubs/junctions so maps branch and loop; chunk sizes may be larger than the
   catalog says (bigger is better: hubs ~20 x 20 u). CH-01 is the spawn hub: 8 enemy slots on a ring of radius
   ~7 u around the beacon at the centre, beacon dais 3 x 3 u, 4 sockets.
2. `tools/validate-chunks.mjs` (Node 24+, ESM, no packages): validates the file against CHUNK_FORMAT.md - schema
   and enums, unique ids, exactly 25 chunks, at least one hub with 8 enemy slots and a beacon,
   box sanity (min < max), sockets on the footprint edge with a platform touching the edge across the full width at
   the socket height, socket width >= 3, walkway width rule (every platform used for walking: smaller horizontal
   extent >= 1.0 and larger >= 3.0; hubs/junctions have at least one platform >= 6 x 6), stacking clearance >= 4.0 u
   between XZ-overlapping platforms that are not in contact (touching neighbours, where one's top equals the
   other's bottom or they share a side, are fine), gift/altar slot rules, and INTERNAL REACHABILITY: build a graph of
   platforms (edge if they touch/overlap horizontally within 0.05 u and the height step <= 0.55 u, or a jump fits the
   design hop-envelope table measured edge-to-edge, or a JUMP_PAD can reach a platform whose top is <= pad top + 4 u
   within 5 u horizontally); every socket platform, gift slot platform and altar slot platform must be reachable from
   every socket. Print a per-chunk summary (size, sockets, gift slots, height range) and exit 1 on any error.
3. Run your validator mentally against your data; prefer simple, clearly valid geometry over clever shapes.

## Constraints
Files: `design/chunks.json`, `tools/validate-chunks.mjs`. Original names only; never mention any other game.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `node tools/validate-chunks.mjs` exits 0 on your catalog and reports clear errors on broken data.
- [ ] Chunks deliver elevation (stairs, towers, drops, two-level bridges, jump-pad lifts) and wide paths.
- [ ] NOTES: per-chunk one-line summary (size, sockets, height range, gift slots).
