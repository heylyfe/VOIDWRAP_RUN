## Task
1. Enemies by behaviour: `enemyInfo.get(id).kind` indexes `ENEMIES` (content.ts); its `params.behavior` is
   "chaser" | "turret" | "mimic" | "corrupter" (missing = chaser). Pooled enemy groups switch which model is visible
   per frame (all four models pre-built per pool slot; unit radius, the group is scaled by the enemy radius as now):
   - chaser: the existing `Chaser`.
   - turret (`Turret.tsx`): a squat pylon/eye that turns to face the local player, glows brighter just before
     firing is not knowable - just a slow pulse.
   - mimic (`Mimic.tsx`): while `state === ENEMY_DISGUISED` it must look EXACTLY like a normal gift (reuse
     `GiftBox`, same size as real gifts regardless of the radius scale); when hunting it becomes a toothy dark box
     with the enemy glow.
   - corrupter (`Corrupter.tsx`): a ragged wisp with a dark core and drifting motes.
   - Every enemy reads clearly as dormant while `state === ENEMY_DORMANT` (dimmed emissive, slow idle) vs hunting.
2. Projectiles (`Projectiles.tsx`): pooled glowing bolts (max 64) at `projectilePositions(now)`, bloom-friendly.
3. Crumbling tiles: `store.tiles` lists platforms in TILE_WARN or TILE_GONE. In `Platforms.tsx`, PLATFORM_CRUMBLE
   platforms get a visibly cracked/different top at all times (players must recognise them before stepping on);
   TILE_WARN platforms shake slightly and flash; TILE_GONE platforms are hidden (or a faint ghost outline). Keep the
   existing look for normal platforms; avoid re-creating geometry when tiles change (toggle visibility/uniforms).
4. Altars (`Altar.tsx`): for each `level.altars`, a small stone shrine with a floating sigil that glows; when its id
   is in `store.altarsUsed` it goes dark. A floating "E" prompt appears when the local player is within 2 units.
5. Class ability visuals for the LOCAL and remote players (remote: only the class colour accent is needed):
   grappler - a thin glowing line from the player to the grapple target while `abilityActiveFrames > 0`;
   blinker - a brief flash/afterimage at the blink origin when a blink starts (detect the frame where
   `abilityActiveFrames` jumps up); glider - small wing/streamer planes that open while gliding (airborne, falling,
   ability held: read `playerController()`; falling = vel.y < 0 and not grounded). Striker keeps `DashStreak`.
6. Ice event (`level.event === EVENT_ICE`): platforms get a glossy pale-blue sheen (tint + higher specular) - a
   pure function `iceTint(baseColor)` in a `.ts` file with a test.
7. Wire everything into `GameClient.tsx` (World component). Keep all existing behaviour (spectator camera, golden
   gifts, themes, effects, FPS probe).
