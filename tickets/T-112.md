**Coordinate through the orchestrator with:** T-107 (validation framework seam),

## 1. Spawn safety
Gifts still end up inside platforms, jump pads and other objects.

Root-cause why the existing validity check misses these cases — the likely gap is
that the check is applied to the chunk/pattern gift slots but **not** to
pattern-engine spawn points, and that it tests only platform *tops* rather than
solid volumes.

Then make the check universal: **no gift may spawn intersecting any solid or
hazard volume** — platforms, paths, ramps, jump pads, trampolines, tripmines,
pedestals, altars, enemy spawn markers. Use spatial rejection sampling with a
guaranteed fallback position so a level can never fail to place its gifts.

Note the existing convention before writing the check: non-elevated gift slots
sit at `platform_top + 0.3` (455 of 552 existing slots), elevated ones up to
`+0.85`. A naive "must be within 0.05 of the top" check rejects almost all valid
content — that mistake was already made once in this repo.

Harness: assert zero intersecting gifts across 5,000 seeds.

## 2. Straggler highlight
When **<= 20%** of a level's gifts remain uncollected, every remaining gift gets
highlighted: a distinct beacon pulse/beam visible through fog, clearly different
from normal glow, golden gifts, and LURE's glimmer (JUDGMENT CALL 2). This ends
the "wandering an empty level for one gift" phase.

Threshold is data-driven in the content pipeline, not a magic number in code.

## Done when
Harness asserts zero intersecting gifts; the <=20% highlight triggers reliably in
tests and a playthrough; unconfusable with LURE or golden gifts; gates green.

## Autonomous choices
- Rejection sampling with a fallback rather than failing the level: a level that
  cannot place a gift must still be playable, and the harness will catch a
  fallback that fires too often.
- The highlight is a beam/pulse, not a colour change: colour is already carrying
  normal-vs-golden, so a third colour would collide with T-109's palette.
