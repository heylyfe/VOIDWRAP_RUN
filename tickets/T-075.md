# Ticket T-075: enemy batch B - Blight, Metronome, Overlook, Chorus, Coil, and the Lure redesign

Implement five enemies plus the Lure redesign from the frozen registry (`design/ENEMY_REGISTRY.md`).
Batch A (Rewind, Warden, Peal, Kite, Static) is being written at the same time by another agent in
different files. Never read or edit their modules; if you need a shared helper, put it in
`server/crates/sim/src/enemies/mod.rs` and say so in NOTES.

## What exists (verified)

- `server/crates/sim/src/rules.rs` holds the enemy model: `BEHAVIOR_*` constants (0..8), `EnemyParams`,
  `Brain` (one variant per behaviour), one `*Brain` struct per enemy with a phase enum and timers, and
  `Brain::for_behavior`.
- `design/content.json` already has registry entries for all six (added by T-072).
- The sim crate is compiled to WASM: no wall clock, no `sin`/`cos`/`powf` in the step path, only
  `+ - * / sqrt`. Determinism is mandatory.

## The six behaviours

1. **Blight** (`BEHAVIOR_BLIGHT`, cue 3, min level 8, cap 1). Squelching approach. A hit INFECTS: the
   victim is slowed, and the infection PAUSES the timers of Rewind and Kite (synergy). Cleansed by a
   jump-pad launch or a support-class ability.
2. **Metronome** (`BEHAVIOR_METRONOME`, cue 1, min level 10, cap 1). A beacon shows a number that ticks
   up; the marked player must use their class ability exactly on that number or be struck. One class
   never attracts it (see the registry).
3. **Overlook** (`BEHAVIOR_OVERLOOK`, cue 2, min level 12, cap 1). Teleports around a marked player
   leaving a glowing mark, then fires a slow tracking laser that ONLY the marked player can see and be
   harmed by. Teammates can draw its path during the collapse phase.
4. **Chorus** (`BEHAVIOR_CHORUS`, cue 3, min level 10, cap 1). Spawns only when two "small" permanents
   exist and level >= 10. Instruments spawn as special gifts; each one collected freezes Chorus and
   extends its timer. When the timer runs out, Chorus moves and hunts.
5. **Coil** (`BEHAVIOR_COIL`, cue 1, min level 4, cap 2). Coils, leaps to random map spots, and emits
   shockwaves that inflict OVERTUNED - the debuff that makes Peal lethal. Deliberate pair with Peal.
6. **Lure** (`BEHAVIOR_LURE`, cue 1, min level 3, cap 2, tags fog/ambush/collapse) - the redesign of
   the enemy that used to do nothing. Keep its star silhouette and purple-fog identity:
   - DORMANT: sits in fog emitting a soft glimmer similar to gift glow, so at gameplay distance it
     reads as a collectible. The fog pulses slowly around it - that pulse IS the telegraph. No
     movement, no chase.
   - TRIGGER: a player inside a short radius gets a brief flare-up (tier 1, under 1 s), then a small
     shockwave, and it pursues the nearest player for a few seconds before re-dorming elsewhere.
   - It is the ambush layer: distinct from Static (hunts) and Coil (random leaps). Its whole threat is
     being mistaken for a gift.
   - One class sees the true silhouette through the glimmer; a jump-pad launch escapes the pursuit.
   - During the collapse phase it re-dorms aggressively when players rush the exit.

## Requirements

- One module per enemy under `server/crates/sim/src/enemies/`, with `mod.rs` re-exporting them and
  `rules.rs` wiring the new `Brain` variants. If you keep them in `rules.rs`, say why in NOTES.
- OVERTUNED: batch A adds the flag and timer to the player state. If it is not there yet when you
  start, add it yourself in a way that is easy to merge (a single field plus a helper), and say so in
  NOTES. Coil inflicts it; Peal reads it.
- Synergy hooks are declarative in the registry; implement the effects they name, not new ad-hoc rules.
- Every new public item gets a doc comment. Telegraphs must be visible at gameplay distance.
- Tests per enemy: happy path, counter/escape working, and the telegraph lasting the tier's minimum.
  Deterministic, no sleeps.

## Output format

Complete files with `=== FILE: <path> ===` ... `=== END FILE ===`, or single functions with
`=== FN: <path> :: <name> ===` ... `=== END FN ===`. Allowed paths: anything under
`server/crates/sim/src/`, plus `protocol/schema.json` if a client-visible field is genuinely needed.

Do not touch `web/`, `design/`, or `server/crates/server/`. No new dependencies.

## Done when

`cargo test -p sim` passes with the new tests, `cargo clippy -p sim --all-targets -- -D warnings` is
clean, and each of the six has a working telegraph and counter. Put the tuning numbers you chose in
NOTES - balancing happens later.
