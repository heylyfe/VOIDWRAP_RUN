## Verdict

**PASS.** 19 of 21 lines green. The two non-green lines are both parked with
reasons and neither is a regression introduced by this wave:

1. **`5.heights` 4.47 %** — pre-existing, lives in the frozen `design/chunks.json`.
   Improved from 6.02 % incidentally by the T-107 design fixes. Needs a human
   call on the frozen contract.
2. **playthrough `level-progression`** — a harness limitation (the bot cannot
   navigate ramps), not a game defect. The 7 checks that do run all pass, and
   the voting kit's real mode passes 3/3 against the same server.

**One finding the owner must read: F-115-4.** `just verify` invokes
`voting-conformance`, `playthrough`, and `matchmaker-conformance` with no URL,
and all three print "skipped" and exit 0. The gate has been reporting green
while three of its checks never ran. This is not a regression from this wave —
it has always been true — but it means "gates green" in this repo is weaker
than it reads. Fixing it is a contract change (a new `just verify-live` recipe),
so it is parked for a human decision rather than changed unilaterally.
