=== NOTES ===
M1 visual QA — findings, by severity (screenshot # cited; only visible evidence):

HIGH
1. #01, #06: platform top faces render blown-out pastel cyan/pink, not the intended #3b2a6b/#1f4b7a/#5a1f5e/#20565a — kills the dark-chromatic "void" identity, reads as ice-cream toys. Fix: darken top-face albedo/light toward the four family hues, keep sides near-black, let top edges catch a thin cyan rim for separation.
2. #01: the "red spiky octahedron" enemy renders salmon/peach, not #ff4d4d — threat reads as a gift/ribbon decoration next to gold boxes. Fix: push albedo/emissive to #ff4d4d, saturate, darken spike tips; if possible slightly larger silhouette.

MEDIUM
3. #01, #06: exit beacon is a barely visible dark smudge above spawn — players can't spot the goal. Fix: raise opacity, give it a faint cyan tint or a low-intensity base ring (no bloom required); acceptable if it stays dim but must read as a vertical structure.
4. #01: oversized translucent "LEVEL 1" ghost text overlaps player and platforms mid-screen, looking like a render artifact. Fix: shorter fade, smaller size, or move it above the action as a brief opaque banner.
5. #01, #06: bottom-right gift cluster is clipped by the screen edge and piled onto/overlapping the enemy — a collectible is effectively off-camera. Fix: pull the cluster fully into camera view; separate gift and enemy spawns.
6. #02: mid-fall frame is ~95% empty black with a tiny figure — no sense of falling or danger. Fix: simple cues that aren't post-processing — downward speed streaks, subtle darkening vignette, or slight camera drift/roll.

LOW
7. #01/#02/#05/#06: HUD "■ 5 / 5" never says remaining vs collected (death screen later says "Gifts collected 0"). Fix: label it, e.g. "GIFTS LEFT 5/5".
8. #05: gameplay HUD (LEVEL 1 / 5 / 5) is still dimly visible behind the RUN OVER panel. Fix: hide HUD while the summary panel is open.
9. #01/#02/#05/#06: tiny "server: connected · tick · ping · players" debug line always visible bottom-left. Fix: gate behind a debug flag or drop to only fatal states.
10. #00: title screen is barren near-black for a "maximalist, chromatic" brief; wide gap in "VOIDWRAP  RUN" reads like a mis-kern. Fix: add a faint chromatic backdrop (platform/gift silhouettes or a low gradient), tighten wordmark spacing.

KEEP (already good):
A. Blocky magenta-shirt player silhouette reads instantly on both platforms and against the void (#01/#02/#06).
B. Top-centre HUD pill is crisp, high-contrast, legible at speed.
C. Death screen hierarchy: clear RUN OVER heading, readable stats, high-contrast magenta "Run again" CTA with cyan secondary link clearly de-emphasised.

Caveats: #3 may be intentional pre-collection dimming (can't verify intent from stills); #2's peach cast could be lighting, but the visible result fails the palette either way. No finding on missing bloom/CA — acknowledged as planned, out of scope.
