# T-124 — Platform-graph routing for the playthrough bot: PARKED

**Cycle 12, refinement 1 of 3.** The top candidate from Cycle 11: the last three
dark checks (`level-progression`, `vote`, `shop`) all hang off the bot
completing a level.

## What was built

`tools/playthrough/run.mjs` gained a platform graph:

- `maxGapForRise(rise)` mirrors `sim::levelgen::design_max_gap` — 6.0 u flat,
  4.0 u at +2.0 u, 8.0 u at −4.0 u, nothing above +2.0 u.
- `buildPlatformGraph` links two platforms when the edge-to-edge gap fits the
  envelope in either direction.
- `platformUnder` / `nearestOn` / `nextHop` give a BFS route from the platform
  the bot stands on to the platform holding its target gift.
- `steer` aims at the next platform's nearest point instead of straight at the
  gift, and `nearestLanding` gives air control while airborne.

## Measured — no win

Five 60-second runs, 8 clients, fresh server each time:

| build | gifts collected (best client, of 88) | deaths |
| --- | --- | --- |
| before the graph (T-121) | 39 | 8 |
| graph + air control | 41, 35, 33 | 6, 8, 8 |
| graph, jump probe capped at 4.0 u | 37, 29 | 6, 8 |
| graph, landing search capped at 4 u below | 28 | 8 |

The spread between runs is larger than the difference between builds. **The
graph did not produce a measurable win**, and the bots still die to the void
(`cause=0`) in every run.

## Why it is parked rather than pushed further

The deaths are not from the jump probe. Capping the probe range, capping the
drop it will accept, and adding air control all left the death count at 6–8 of
8. That means the bots are leaving the level somewhere the probe does not
cover, and guessing at the cause from the outside is exactly the grinding the
run discipline forbids.

The next attempt needs a measurement first: log the position and velocity of
each `DEATH_VOID` on the server side, then look at where they actually fall.
That is a server change, not a harness change, and it is the right first step.

## What is kept

The graph code stays. It is correct, it is the foundation the next attempt
needs, and it does not regress the kit — `level-progression` was already a
named known failure and still is. `--known-failures level-progression` stays in
`tools/verify-live.mjs`.

## Tuning notes

- 88 gifts across 75 platforms with 8 bots is a lot of ground for 60 s even
  with perfect routing. If the next attempt gets close, raise the window before
  loosening the check.
- The bots collect 7 gifts in the first second and 21 by second four, then
  plateau. Whatever kills them happens after the first few seconds, not at
  spawn.
