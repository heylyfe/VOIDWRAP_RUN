## 1. Context

The pattern audit reports that 17.63% of generated levels share their exact pattern set

## 3. Hard rules — a violation fails the ticket

1. **Footprint extent in [10, 20] u.** The footprint is the XZ bounding box of all
   platforms; the extent is the larger of `max_x - min_x` and `max_z - min_z`. Aim for
   14–18.
2. **At least one socket.** A socket's `pos` lies **exactly on the footprint edge** on
   the side it faces, and `pos.y` is a platform top at that socket. Facing is
   `N = +Z`, `E = +X`, `S = -Z`, `W = -X`, pointing **out** of the pattern.
3. **Socket width** is an integer `>= 3` and `<= 8`. Junctions need `>= 6`.
4. **A platform whose top is at a socket's `y` must touch that edge across the full
   socket width, centred on the socket.** Two joined patterns must form a continuous
   walkway.
5. **Ramps**: `rise / run <= 0.5`, `run >= 2.0`, `width >= 3.0`. Stairs do not exist.
   Ramp kinds are `RAMP_PX`, `RAMP_NX`, `RAMP_PZ`, `RAMP_NZ` (the suffix is the
   direction of travel).
6. **Stacking**: two platforms that overlap in XZ need `>= 4.0` u of vertical clearance
   between the lower one's top and the upper one's bottom.
7. **Adjacent platform tops** that touch must differ by either `~0` (a flat
   continuation) or **between 1.0 and 6.0 u**. A 0.5 u half-step is a violation.
8. **Tags**: at least one `theme:` and at least one `mech:`; at most two `theme:` and
   at most three `mech:`. Values come from the closed vocabularies in §4.
9. **`min_level`** is 1–12. **`difficulty`** is 1–3. Do not hard-code a level number
   anywhere else.
10. **Ids** are `pa2_<slug>`, lowercase `[a-z0-9_]`, unique across your batch.
11. **No new platform kinds, no new tag values, no new fields.**

## 4. Closed vocabularies

`theme:` — `ember`, `void`, `frost`, `ruin`, `garden`, `machine`, `spire`, `hollow`

`mech:` — `climb`, `drop`, `span`, `bounce`, `crawl`, `gauntlet`, `rest`, `loot`,
`fork` (junction only), `dead_end` (dead_end only)

## 6. Output format

Reply with **one block per pattern** and nothing else. No prose before, between or after
the blocks.

```
=== NEW PATTERN ===
{
  "id": "pa2_ember_switchback",
  "name": "Ember Switchback",
  "role": "path",
  "min_level": 3,
  "difficulty": 2,
  "tags": ["theme:ember", "mech:climb", "mech:span"],
  "platforms": [ ... ],
  "sockets": [ ... ],
  "gift_slots": [ ... ],
  "enemy_slots": [],
  "altar_slots": [],
  "beacon": null
}
```

Rules for the block body:

- It is a JSON object. It must parse.
- Include every key shown above. `enemy_slots` is `[]` and `beacon` is `null` for
  non-hub patterns.
- `gift_slots` entries are `{"pos": [x, y, z], "elevated": true|false}`. A gift slot's
  `pos` must lie within a platform's XZ bounds and its `y` must be the platform top
  (or up to 0.5 above it when `elevated` is true).
- `altar_slots` entries are `[x, y, z]` on a platform top.
- `sockets` entries are `{"id": "a", "pos": [x, y, z], "facing": "N|E|S|W", "width": n}`.

## 7. How this is checked

The orchestrator will, for each block:

1. Parse the JSON. A parse error fails that pattern.
2. Check the role/socket-count mix against §2.
3. Recompute the footprint extent and require `10 <= extent <= 20`.
4. Re-check every socket against rules 2–4, every ramp against rule 5, every stacked
   pair against rule 6, every adjacent top pair against rule 7, and the tag counts
   against rule 8.
5. Append the survivors to `design/patterns-a.json` and run
   `node tools/validate-content.mjs` plus the pattern audit. `5.ramps` must stay at 0
   violations and `0.input` must stay clean.

A pattern that fails a check is dropped and reported; the rest of the batch still
lands. Aim for all 15.

15 new patterns are emitted, at least 12 survive the checks, and the pattern audit's
