## F-114-1 — Ramp-to-ramp teleport (SEVERE, FIXED)

**Repro:** stand at the foot of a ramp, walk up it, and continue into the foot
of a second ramp that meets it at a shared edge. The player is snapped back to
the FIRST ramp's low edge — a teleport of several metres, backwards.

**Root cause (two defects in the step-up path, `sim` controller):**
1. `overlaps_any_solid` treated a ramp as solid whenever the box bottom sat
   below the surface by more than `GROUND_EPSILON` (0.001). On a slope the box
   bottom is *always* below the surface by more than that, so every ramp was
   solid to the step-up probe.
2. The step-up lifted only to the *triggering* platform's surface. When two
   ramps meet, the correct surface is the highest one under the box, not the
   one that happened to trigger.

**Fix:** tolerance is now `STEP_HEIGHT` (0.55, the walkable step limit); new
`highest_step_surface(world, pos, feet)` returns the highest surface under the
box within one step.

**Tests added** (`controller::m3_tests`, fixtures `ramp_wide_level`,
`ramp_to_ramp_level`):
- `standing_still_on_a_ramp_does_not_slide`
- `walking_across_a_ramp_keeps_ground_contact`
- `landing_on_a_ramp_mid_slope_settles_on_the_surface`
- `two_ramps_meeting_at_a_shared_edge_are_walked_through`

**Commit:** `f4f8b3f`

## F-114-2 — Ramps generate facing the wrong way (SEVERE, FIXED)

**Repro:** a generated ramp's high edge is at the low end of the path it is
supposed to join; walking "up" it drops you.

**Root cause:** the rotation/anchor logic derived the incline direction from
the platform's own min/max without checking which end actually met a walkable
surface.

**Fix:** orientation is derived from the anchor, and a generation-time
assertion rejects backwards ramps.

**Commit:** `8c5e66b`

## F-114-3 — 7.7 % of ramps rise into nothing (SEVERE, FIXED)

**Repro (harness):** dump 6,000 levels and count ramps with no walkable
surface within one step of either end.

**Measured before:** **9,819 / 127,635 ramps (7.693 %)** orphaned, across
**3,788 / 6,000 levels (63 %)**. A player who walks up one of these reaches a
lip with nothing behind it — the geometry promises a route that does not exist.

**Fix:** `levelgen.rs` gained `RAMP_ANCHOR_TOLERANCE` (**0.55 = the walkable
step limit**, deliberately the same constant as the controller's `STEP_HEIGHT`
so generation and movement agree), `RAMP_ANCHOR_OVERLAP = 0.25`, and
`pub fn orphaned_ramps(&[Platform]) -> Vec<usize>`. Nine design fragments were
corrected in `design/` (ch06, ch08, ch21, ch22, ch25, `pa_ruin_switchback`,
`pb_machine_span`).

**Measured after:** **0 / 125,871 ramps across 0 levels.**

**Note on ch04_twin_terrace:** its 0.5 u lip was initially "fixed" by lowering
the deck to 3.5, which broke the 4.0 u `STACK_CLEARANCE` rule and produced 52
`no_plan` fallbacks. Reverted — the 0.5 u lip is a legal step under the
corrected tolerance. **Lesson: the tolerance was the bug, not the geometry.**

**Commit:** `d10c30c`

## F-114-4 — 1.96 % of gifts spawn inside solids (SEVERE, FIXED)

**Repro (harness):** dump 6,000 levels, test every gift's AABB against every
platform volume.

**Measured before:** **14,783 / 755,887 (1.956 %)** gifts intersecting a solid.
A gift inside a platform is uncollectable, which softlocks the collect phase.

**Root cause:** the validity check only rejected the *supporting* platform, so
a gift could sit inside any other platform that happened to overlap the slot.

**Fix:** `accept_candidate` widened to every platform except the supporting one.

**Measured after:** **8 / 755,887 (0.0011 %)** — a 1,848x reduction. The
residual 8 are the guaranteed-fallback positions (a level where every candidate
was rejected must still place its gifts).

**Commit:** `8c5e66b` (fix), `d324df2` (harness metric `5.gift-spawn-safety`)

## F-114-6 — Enemy hitboxes vs telegraphs (MEDIUM, NOT INVESTIGATED)

The registry declares a telegraph tier per enemy; the hitbox radius is a
separate constant. No test asserts that a tier-1 enemy's strike lands inside
the volume its telegraph drew. **Not investigated** — the harness has no
per-enemy strike-volume probe.

**Disposition: PARKED.** Concrete next step for a future run: add a
`strike_volume` field to the registry and assert `telegraph_radius >=
strike_radius` in `validate-content`.
