# Ticket T-055 r8a: plain jumps may climb at most 2.0 u (2.25 is the apex - impossible in practice)
Target: INKLING (max effort)

The edge-feasibility physics test (`server/crates/sim/tests/playthrough.rs`, `every_graph_edge_kind_is_physically_doable`)
now plays every kind of graph edge with the real controller. Plain jumps that climb +2.25 u fail (gap 0.50/1.50/1.75/
2.00, 130 edges): 2.25 u is exactly the jump apex (12^2 / (2*32)), so the feet never clear the ledge. Fix the RULES and
the DATA, not the test:
1. `server/crates/sim/src/levelgen.rs` (FN edits): a plain (non-pad) jump edge needs rise <= 2.0 u (apex - 0.25);
   keep the other rules (landing >= 2 u, gap <= envelope - 1 u, touching drops fine). Adjust the hop table use so
   no rise above 2.0 is admitted without a pad.
2. `design/chunks.json` (FILE, one chunk per line): every chunk whose internal climbs need +2.25 (e.g. the two-level
   bridge's interior jump steps, the high road entry) gets an extra 0.5 u step or a lower rise so every climb is
   <= 2.0 u, keeping the chunk's character and its gift slots valid.
3. `tools/validate-chunks.mjs` (FILE): same rule.
Then `node tools/validate-chunks.mjs` passes and the levelgen tests stay green.
