# Ticket T-055 r9: jump-pad targets may be at most +3.5 u (the pad apex is 4.0)
Target: INKLING (max effort)

Jump pads now launch correctly (controller fixed). The edge-feasibility physics test passes EVERY bucket except pad
launches that climb exactly +4.00 u (16 buckets, e.g. gap 0.00/0.75/1.00/2.25/3.00/3.25 rise +4.00): the pad apex is
16^2 / (2*32) = 4.0 u (3.87 u at 60 Hz), so a +4.0 landing can never be cleared. Same class of mistake as the old
+2.25 plain-jump rule. Fix the RULES and the DATA (not the test):
1. `server/crates/sim/src/levelgen.rs` (FN edits): a jump-pad edge requires rise <= 3.5 u (apex - 0.5), horizontal
   reach unchanged (<= 5.0 u), landing >= 2 u.
2. `design/chunks.json` (FILE, one chunk per line): pad lifts (the jump-pad lift, the high silo, the switchyard, any
   other pad chunk) get stage heights of <= 3.5 u above the pad top (e.g. 3.5 u stages; add a stage where needed to
   keep the total climb), sockets/gift slots/clearances still valid.
3. `tools/validate-chunks.mjs` (FILE): same pad rule.
Then `node tools/validate-chunks.mjs` passes and the levelgen tests stay green.
