# T-128 — Reference scale: name the patterns that own the outlier budget

**Cycle:** 13 · **Agent:** orchestrator · **Status:** PASS (3 of 6 fixed, 3 documented as ramp-forced)

## Why

`5.reference-scale` passed at **3.85%** against a 10% ceiling, but the outlier
budget was not spread out — **six patterns accounted for all 8,081 out-of-band
placements**. An aggregate rate cannot see that: one pattern could be 3x the
reference scale and the metric would still pass as long as the rest were clean.
The owner's directive #2 was "map generation has inconsistencies".

## Measurement

Per-pattern max XZ footprint extent, 400 seeds x 3 player counts x 5 levels:

|---|---|---|---|
| `ch06_descent_of_sighs` | 30.00 u | [10, 20] | 50% over |
| `ch15_tri_crown` | 25.00 u | [10, 20] | 25% over |
| `ch04_twin_terrace` | 22.00 u | [10, 20] | 10% over |

## The structural constraint nobody had written down

A chunk that drops `d` needs `2 * d / 0.5` of ramp (the `RAMP_MAX_RISE_RUN` cap)
plus its decks. That makes the footprint a *consequence* of the vertical design,
not a free choice:

- `ch04_twin_terrace` drops 5.0 u -> 8 u ramp + 6 u deck + 8 u ramp = **22 u minimum**
- `ch06_descent_of_sighs` drops 5.5 u -> 10 + 6 + 10 + 2 + 2 = **30 u minimum**
- `ch15_tri_crown` drops 6.5 u -> **26 u minimum** (it measures 25 u, i.e. already tighter than forced)

So `ch04` and `ch06` cannot enter the band without either reducing their drop
(a redesign of the piece's identity) or breaking the ramp slope cap. Per the
standing order — "polish, never redesign", "park anything destructive" — they are
**not** reshaped. They are documented instead.

## Change

1. **Fixed the three with no structural excuse:**
   - `ch25_hanging_gallery` 21 -> 20 u: base and upper gallery trimmed 1 u. The ramp
     (0..9, 4.5 rise over 9 u run = exactly the cap) is untouched; both sockets sit
     at x = 10.5 so they stay centred; every gift slot stays on the deck.
   - `ch09_jump_pad_lift` 8 -> 10 u: base deck widened east. The south socket at
     `[4, 0, 0]` stays on the south edge.
   - `ch16_misers_loft` 8 -> 10 u: same treatment. The altar slot at `[6.5, 0, 6]`
     and the south socket are unaffected.
2. **The metric now explains itself.** `5.reference-scale` reports the top six
   patterns by placement count with their extent, vertical drop, and the footprint
   their drop forces (`2 * drop / RAMP_MAX_RISE_RUN`). That separates "out of band
   because it is a tall set-piece" from "out of band because someone typed the
   wrong number" — the distinction the aggregate rate was hiding.

## Tuning notes

- `ch06_descent_of_sighs` still carries 8 u of slack beyond its forced 22 u. That
  slack is the long sightline down the descent — deliberate, but if the owner ever
  wants it tighter, trimming the two 2 u end decks to 1 u each gets it to 28 u.
- The `[10, 20] u` band and the `0.5` ramp slope cap are in tension for any piece
  dropping more than 3 u. A future cycle that wants more big set-pieces should
  widen the band rather than flatten the ramps.

## Choice log

- **Fix three, document three.** Reshaping `ch04`/`ch06` means changing their drop,
  which changes what the piece *is*. That is a redesign, and the standing order
  parks redesigns.
- **Report the forced minimum rather than adding a second gate.** A second gate at
  32 u would have been theatre — nothing violates it. Naming the offenders and
  their structural floor makes the existing gate honest instead.
- **`ch15` left alone despite being 25 u.** Its forced minimum is 26 u, so it is
  already tighter than the constraint allows. Trimming it would mean deleting the
  west low arm, which is the junction's whole point.
