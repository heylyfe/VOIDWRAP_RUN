=== NOTES ===
Balance intent:
- Enemies are chaser-only param sets (per ticket). Speeds 2.5-8.4 u/s, all under the 9 u/s run and far under the 28 u/s dash; grace 2.0-5.0 s; aggro 6.5-34 u. Archetypes spread: huge-aggro slow stalker (Pallbearer), fast short-range ambusher (Snapshrike), tiny quick one (Moteling), big slow wall (Rift Warden), mid-range all-rounder (Gale Wight), early-waking long-range pressure (Lantern Leech).
- Threat shown in lobby so the pre-run pick is informed: weak/ignorable (1) through draft-around-it (4-5).
- Upgrades: commons are ~+10-20% single-axis power; rares change the rules (extra mid-air jump, extra life, floatier gravity).
- Curses stay inside playability floors: weakest run_speed 0.88 (floor 0.8), jump_mul 0.9 (floor 0.85), gravity 1.18 normal / 1.25 greater (ceiling 1.25). No card stacks two movement penalties, so 1.5-u steps and 4-u gaps stay makeable.
- Greater curses (The Hollow Choir, Leaden Sky, World of Teeth) sit in the 0.4-2.0 band and are meant to be drafted only as a deliberate risk; World of Teeth can outrun a base player but not a dashing one. All greater curses flagged "rare".
- Swarm Seed / The Hollow Choir add copies of a random drafted enemy; no new spawn logic implied.
- Grades: fluxbloom is the only theme with scanlines (fits its hot CRT-haze mood); slagstone is the coldest/hardest contrast with a blood-red vignette; tidegrave is darkest and softest (underwater); ashfall sits between with mild grain and storm-shimmer aberration.
Unsure / gaps:
- No "greater" rarity value exists in the stated vocabulary, so greater curses reuse "rare".
- "Draft reward" economy for curses is described in text only, as instructed; no reward values invented here.
- extra_lives_add / air_jumps_add integers follow the 0-2 / integer rules; no stacking caps defined by ticket, so none invented.
- Gift/beacon radii have no stated base units, so those mods are multipliers only.
- Enemy behavior lines include speed class, wake (grace) timing and counter as required; all under 110 chars.
- Counts: 6 enemies, 10 upgrades, 10 normal + 3 greater curses, 4 grades; only vocabulary keys used in mods.
=== END NOTES ===
