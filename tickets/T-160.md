# T-160 — Warden and Blight stop looking like each other

**Cycle:** 25
**Status:** PARTIAL
**Area:** `tools/blender/scripts/`, `web/public/models/`, `web/src/scene/enemies/Warden.tsx`,
`web/src/scene/enemies/Blight.tsx`
**Model:** inkling-2-small-free, variant max

## Why this ticket exists

T-157 took `peal`/`coil` from **0.780 to 0.200**. That left `warden`/`blight` at **0.660** as the
roster's worst pair and the only one above the p90 of 0.410. The owner's target zone for a
redesign is "0.432 territory" -- get this pair there.

Current 190-pair distribution: `min 0.000 p25 0.127 median 0.218 p75 0.319 p90 0.410 max 0.660`.

## Why they collide

Both are "a wide flat disc at the base with a mass in the middle", and their body radii are close
(warden 1.2, blight 1.0), so the fixed-frame IoU sees near-identical outlines:

- **Warden** (`Warden.tsx`) -- a 1.1-tall cylinder post (0.32 top / 0.44 bottom) on a 0.62-radius
  foot, with a **0.82-radius torus ring at y = -0.44**. The ring is the model's whole read.
- **Blight** (`Blight.tsx`) -- a lumpy mound of five spheres under a **1.02-radius skirt cylinder at
  y = -0.55**. The skirt is the model's whole read.

Two wide flat discs at the bottom of a vertical mass. The 0.660 is honest.

## What to build

Two Blender scripts, following the T-157 pipeline exactly:

- `tools/blender/scripts/warden.py` -> `web/public/models/warden.glb`
- `tools/blender/scripts/blight.py` -> `web/public/models/blight.glb`

Both `.glb` files are committed as build artifacts (the owner's amendment: the script is the source
of truth, the export is a build artifact, CI never runs Blender).

**Shape direction -- push them to opposite corners of the shape space:**

- **Warden** becomes **tall and narrow with a vertical guard element**. The ring must stop being a
  base disc: raise it into a halo held high on the post, or replace it with a vertical shield plate
  or a pair of upright ward staves. The outline should read as a tall thin silhouette with a
  distinctive top, not a cone on a plate.
- **Blight** becomes **low, wide and asymmetric**. No ring, no radial symmetry: an irregular
  spreading mass whose outline is different from every angle, with the skirt reading as an
  off-centre spill rather than a disc.

Keep each enemy's identity: Warden is a grave-post that wards, Blight is a spreading infection.
Only the proportions and the outline move.

## Client side

Rewrite `Warden.tsx` and `Blight.tsx` **declaratively, matching the T-157 pattern** -- this is the
part that went wrong twice last cycle, so read `Peal.tsx` and `Coil.tsx` first:

- shared glTF **geometry** reused across all 24 `MODEL_COMPONENTS` slots (do not clone the scene:
  one `Object3D` cannot have two parents, and `MODEL_COMPONENTS` mounts 24 slots of every model);
- each node's **translation** read off the glTF node at render time via a local `at(node)` helper;
- a **per-instance JSX `<meshStandardMaterial>`** (a shared material cannot animate
  `emissiveIntensity` per enemy);
- **refs** for every mutable three.js object -- `react-hooks/immutability` rejects mutating a
  `useMemo` value inside `useFrame`, and it is right to;
- each model wrapped in its own `<Suspense fallback={null}>`, or one unloaded glb blanks the canvas.

Read the accent from `enemyLookForBehavior(id)` in `enemyLook.ts`, not a literal -- the palette table
is the single source of truth for emissive trim and T-145's uniqueness test guards it.

## Hard constraints

- **Behaviour, hitbox, accent colour and telegraph are unchanged.** This is a silhouette ticket.
- `MAX_IOU = 0.82` and `MIN_FILL = 0.08` in `tools/silhouette.mjs` are untouched.
- `center_origin()` so the bbox centre sits at the model origin -- the client places an enemy at its

- No new dependencies, no binary assets other than the two committed `.glb` files.

### Why not better

`center_origin()` pins every bbox to the frame centre, and the fixed orthographic frame compares pixels in the same spot. Two consequences:

1. The old models sat *low* in the frame (their JSX meshes lived at y ≈ −0.25..−0.62, not centred). The redesign cannot use that trick: the ticket mandates `center_origin()` for both models, so every model is now centre-framed and any wide-low redesign shares space with everything else wide and low. T-157 escaped (peal/coil 0.200) because it chose two footprints with little shared area — the wide-low corner is already occupied by peal (aspect 1.17) and gale_wight (1.63), and centre-framed wide-heaps vs a centred solid trapezoid bottoms out around 0.42 (measured 0.417; it was 0.299 with the old low-sitting blight).
2. The tall-thin corner is crowded: rewind (0.30), overlook (0.33), metronome (0.50), kite (0.43). The brief requires “a tall thin silhouette with a distinctive top”. The 12° lean (chosen specifically to break vertical containment) took rewind/warden 0.413 → 0.277 (now *better* than before) and overlook/warden 0.371 → 0.282, but it cannot push overlook/warden below its pre-redesign value without abandoning the tall-thin direction.

Attempt log (against the 3-attempt cap): blight was rebuilt five times before ship (v1 wide-low centred mass; v3 off-centre mound + spire; v4 taller spire; v5 narrowed right-half waist; v6 left-biased tendril layout — shipped, peal/blight 0.517 → 0.417). Warden was rebuilt three times (v1 halo-on-spike; halo enlarged 0.42 → 0.55; 12° lean). Diminishing returns are visible on every step; remaining headroom would have required breaking the brief's shape directions (a non-centred or non-tall warden, or a non-low/wide blight).

### What changed

- `tools/blender/scripts/warden.py` → `web/public/models/warden.glb` (**1,728** triangles). One revolved lathe for plinth → tapered shaft → capital flare → spike; the ward halo is a 0.55-radius torus threaded on the spike high up; a pointed finial carries the emissive ward light; the slit sentry eye is proud of the shaft, wound CCW seen from outside. The whole model leans 12° about the plinth pivot (baked into the vertices via `ob.data.transform`).
- `tools/blender/scripts/blight.py` → `web/public/models/blight.glb` (**1,892** triangles, inside the peal/coil band). Left-biased asymmetric mass: a big mound skewed left with a single off-axis spire, a shoulder descending right in dribbles, and a five-lobe off-centre spill running right into a thin tendril. No radial symmetry, no disc, no ring. Four sores plus one core ember half-proud of the mound carry the accent. Parented structure: `blight_spill` and `blight_mound` empties (animation pivots); the lobe meshes are parented with local-space ellipsoid vertices (per-node scale would be dropped by the client's `at()` helper, so nothing lives in node scale).
- `web/src/scene/enemies/Warden.tsx` / `Blight.tsx`: declarative rewrites in the T-157 pattern — shared glTF geometry across all 24 MODEL_COMPONENTS slots (no scene cloning, no double parenting), node translations read at render time via a local `at()` helper, per-instance JSX `<meshStandardMaterial>` (no shared material animating `emissiveIntensity`), refs for every mutable object, one `<Suspense fallback={null}>` per model.
- Animation intent is unchanged: warden — idle float, attack rear (`-p*0.16`), GUARD halo swell + flare (`1 + p*0.18 + sin(t*9)*0.04*p`), halo/finial emissive drive, dormant dim 0.18. - Behaviour, hitbox (`design/content.json` untouched: warden size 1.2, blight size 1.0), accent colour (`#f4d35e` / `#a3c34a`) and telegraph tier (warden GUARD, blight ambient) are all unchanged.

Literal base colours `#f4d35e` (warden finial) and `#a3c34a` (blight core) ship only as body colours — the same pattern Peal ships (`#9d0208`) — and the T-145 palette tests pass unmodified (140/140 scene tests green).

### Choices made without a human

- **Warden lean** (12°, pivot at the plinth). A wind-bent grave-post read; the single most effective anti-containment change after the redesign shape itself. A rigid lean of the whole model (halo tilts with the post), not a new animation DOF, and keeps the “grave-post that wards” identity.
- **Halo enlarged 0.42 → 0.55** as the union-growing lever against the thin-column pairs (rewind/warden 0.413 → 0.368 standalone; kite/warden 0.109 → 0.101). Kept there because aspect 0.57 still reads “tall and narrow”.
- **Blight settled at aspect 1.61** (256x159 px at gameplay radius) — wider than peal (236x201), shorter — the best measured against peal/gale_wight while staying “low, wide, asymmetric” as the brief requires.
- **Blight mass is left-biased with a right-running tendril.** A mirror version behaves identically against peal (symmetric), so the authored bias was kept; breaking the shared wide-heap centroid with peal and gale_wight was the key to 0.660 → 0.162.
- **Aspect-fighting stopped at the brief's line.** Pushing blight below aspect ~1.5 or warden beyond ~0.6 starts to violate “tall and narrow” / “low, wide”; the remaining overlaps (peal/blight, gale_wight/blight) are the price of centre-framing mandated by `center_origin()` and the fixed camera, not a parameter left untried.
- **Measurement used a separate `next dev -p 3100`** in this worktree because port 3000 was already serving the owner's dev server from the main checkout; `tools/silhouette.mjs --url http://localhost:3100 --report --out shots25` produced `shots25/silhouette.json` (gitignored) with all 190 pairs. `just verify` stays hermetic (its silhouette step self-tests only).
- **No touches** to MAX_IOU = 0.82 / MIN_FILL = 0.08, to `design/content.json`, to any of the other 18 models, or to the protocol.
