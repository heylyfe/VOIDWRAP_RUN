Observations for you to confirm and fix (all bot-side; the maps pass the reachability validator):
1. Narrow stair treads (1.0 u deep, 10-14 u wide) stepping down 0.5 u onto a big platform fail: the steering target
   for an overlapping/touching lower platform must be a point on the lower top that is clearly beyond the current
   tread (>= half-width + 0.3 u past the tread edge in the direction of travel), and the bot must keep going until it
   is grounded on the target, not stop at the shared edge.
2. Long drops (rise -4.5 to -6) between touching platforms: walk off the edge toward the target's interior; do not
   stop at the edge.
3. Jumps onto small targets (3 x 3 jump pad, 1 x 3 ledges) with rise +0.5..+2: aim the takeoff at the target's
   centre line, use a full run-up along the approach direction, hold jump for full height when rising, and steer to
   the target's centre while airborne.
4. If an edge is genuinely impossible with the real controller (prove it with a short trace), list it in NOTES with
   seed/level/platforms - the lead will then tighten the map rules instead.
