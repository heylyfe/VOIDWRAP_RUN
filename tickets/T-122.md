# T-122 — Tripmine coverage: 1 pattern to 4

**Cycle 11, refinement 2 of 4.** The owner asked for tripmines as a hazard in
the Fix & Test-Tools wave. They shipped, and then almost never appeared.

## Measured before

```
PASS — 8.hazard_entities
       hazard:trampoline: 3 patterns tagged, 1 carry a TRAMPOLINE platform (33.3%)
       hazard:tripmine: 1 patterns tagged, 1 carry a TRIPMINE platform (100.0%)
```

One pattern out of 132. The metric passed because it only asks whether a
tagged pattern carries the entity — it never asks whether the tag is common
enough to matter. A hazard on 0.8 % of the catalog is a hazard most runs never
see.

## Change — additive only

Three new patterns in `design/patterns-b.json`, each with a real `TRIPMINE`
platform in the walkable path:

| id | role | min_level | shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pb_mine_corridor` | path | 4 | 4-wide corridor, 6 u of tripmine between two safe decks |
| `pb_mine_plaza` | junction | 6 | 4-way junction, the whole north half is tripmine |
| `pb_mine_terrace` | path | 3 | 6-wide terrace, 4 u of tripmine mid-span |

No existing pattern was touched. Gift slots sit on the safe decks, never on the
tripmine, so the spawn-safety audit has nothing new to reject.

## Measured after

```
PASS — 8.hazard_entities
       hazard:trampoline: 3 patterns tagged, 1 carry a TRAMPOLINE platform (33.3%)
       hazard:tripmine: 4 patterns tagged, 4 carry a TRIPMINE platform (100.0%)
```

Catalog 132 -> 135 patterns. Pattern audit **17/17 PASS**, economy audit
**8/8 PASS**, `validate-content` green.

## Tuning notes

- `hazard:trampoline` is still 3 tagged / 1 carrying the entity (33.3 %). That
  is the same class of thinness tripmine had, and it is the next candidate.
- Tripmine density is now 4/135 = 3.0 % of the catalog. If the owner wants
  minefields to be a real mode flavour, the lever is the Extreme/Chaos
  frequency tags, not more patterns.
- `pb_mine_plaza` puts tripmine on a junction, which is the harshest placement:
  every route through the junction crosses it. Watch whether it reads as
  unfair at difficulty 3.
