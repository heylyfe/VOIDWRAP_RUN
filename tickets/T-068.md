# Ticket T-068: ramps replace every stair (sim core)

Target: INKLING-class effort. This is the crux of the whole overhaul: every later wave builds on it.

## What exists (verified)

- `protocol/schema.json` constants already carry four new platform kinds, generated into
  `server/crates/protocol/src/generated.rs` and `web/src/protocol/generated.ts`:
  `PLATFORM_RAMP_PX = 3`, `PLATFORM_RAMP_NX = 4`, `PLATFORM_RAMP_PZ = 5`, `PLATFORM_RAMP_NZ = 6`.
  `PLATFORM_NORMAL = 0`, `PLATFORM_CRUMBLE = 1`, `PLATFORM_JUMP_PAD = 2`.
- A platform is `Platform { id: u16, kind: u8, bounds: Aabb }`. There is NO slope field and you must
  NOT add one: the ramp direction is encoded in `kind`, so the 48 existing `Platform { .. }`
  constructions across the workspace keep compiling. This is a deliberate, frozen decision.
- Collision lives in `server/crates/sim/src/controller.rs` (`move_axis`, `resting_on_solid`) and
  `server/crates/sim/src/world.rs` (the spatial grid + `solid_bounds`). The sim crate is shared by the
  server and the WASM client, so implementing the slope once here makes server and client agree by
  construction.

## The ramp model (frozen - implement exactly this)

For a platform whose `kind` is one of the four ramp kinds:

- `bounds.min.y` is the surface height at the LOW edge.
- `bounds.max.y` is the surface height at the HIGH edge.
- The surface height at a point is a linear interpolation across the slope axis, clamped to the
  platform's bounds:
  - `PLATFORM_RAMP_PX`: low at `bounds.min.x`, high at `bounds.max.x`, interpolate on x.
  - `PLATFORM_RAMP_NX`: low at `bounds.max.x`, high at `bounds.min.x`, interpolate on x.
  - `PLATFORM_RAMP_PZ`: low at `bounds.min.z`, high at `bounds.max.z`, interpolate on z.
  - `PLATFORM_RAMP_NZ`: low at `bounds.max.z`, high at `bounds.min.z`, interpolate on z.
- A flat platform (kind 0, 1, 2) keeps its current behaviour: surface height is `bounds.max.y`
  everywhere.
- The solid volume of a ramp is everything below its surface. Players never need to go under one.

## Required changes

1. `server/crates/sim/src/world.rs`
   - Add `pub fn surface_top(&self, index: usize, x: f32, z: f32) -> Option<f32>`: the surface height
     of platform `index` at world point `(x, z)`, or `None` when the point is outside its x/z bounds.
     Flat platforms return `bounds.max.y`. Ramps interpolate as above.
   - Add `pub fn is_ramp(kind: u8) -> bool` (free function or associated) covering the four kinds.
   - Keep `solid_bounds` and the grid exactly as they are; the grid still indexes the full AABB.
   - Doc-comment every public item (workspace rule).

2. `server/crates/sim/src/controller.rs`
   - `move_axis`:
     - Y, moving down: land on the surface height at the player's x/z, not blindly on
       `bounds.max.y`. Use the player's centre x/z for the query. Keep the existing flush-push
       behaviour for flat platforms.
     - X/Z: a ramp must not block a player who is standing on or above its surface at the contact
       point. Block only when the player's feet are below the surface height at that point (use a
       small epsilon, e.g. `GROUND_EPSILON`). Flat platforms keep blocking exactly as today.
     - Step-up: when the blocking platform is a ramp, the step-up test must use the surface height
       at the target x/z rather than `bounds.max.y`. The `STEP_HEIGHT` limit still applies to the
       height difference.
   - `resting_on_solid`: compare the feet against the surface height at the player's centre x/z, not
     `bounds.max.y`.
   - Do not change any other behaviour: jump pads, crumble, grapple, dash, the step-up amendment,
     coyote time and the axis order all stay as they are.

3. `server/crates/sim/src/levelgen.rs`
   - `platform_kind_from_str` must accept `"RAMP_PX"`, `"RAMP_NX"`, `"RAMP_PZ"`, `"RAMP_NZ"` and map
     them to the four constants. Keep the existing three names working.

4. Tests (in `controller.rs`'s test module and/or `world.rs`):
   - A player walks up a `RAMP_PZ` from y=0 to y=6 over 15 units without jumping and without losing
     ground contact; assert the final y is within 0.05 of 6.
   - A player walks DOWN the same ramp and stays grounded the whole way (no falling, no bouncing).
   - Walking into the HIGH face of a ramp from the side blocks (you cannot walk through the tall end).
   - A flat platform next to a ramp's high end still needs the existing step-up, and a ramp's low end
     joins a flat platform with no step at all.
   - `surface_top` returns `None` outside the bounds and the exact low/high values at the two edges.
   - The existing staircase test (`staircase_of_half_unit_risers_is_climbable_at_full_speed`) must be
     replaced by a ramp equivalent, or deleted if it only tested stairs. Stairs are gone.
