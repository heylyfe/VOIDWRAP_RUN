# Ticket T-071: pattern batch B (25-50 patterns)

You author PATTERNS against the frozen spec in `design/PATTERN_FORMAT.md`. You do not change the engine,
the spec, or any Rust code. Your output is ONE data file.

## Your file

`design/patterns-b.json`, shape `{ "version": 1, "patterns": [ ... ] }`.

Another agent is authoring `design/patterns-b.json` at the same time. Never read it, never write it,
never reference its ids. Your ids must all start with `pb_` so the two batches can never collide.

## What to author

25 to 50 patterns. Aim for 30. Cover the full space the spec allows:

- Every role: `path`, `junction`, `dead_end`. (Hubs are not yours - the existing hub stays.)
- Every difficulty 1..3, with `min_level` spread from 1 to 12 so late levels get new material.
- Every theme tag in the spec's vocabulary, at least twice each.
- Every mechanic tag, at least twice each: climbs, drops, spans, bounces, crawls, gauntlets, rests,
  loot pockets, forks.
- A real variety of shape: long thin catwalks, wide terraces, two-level stacks, spiral climbs,
  switchbacks, bridges over voids, pillar fields, dead-end caches, elevated loot lofts.
- Height differences between 1 and 6 units per move, matching the reference scale (the player is
  1.8 u tall, platforms are 10-20 u across, ramps rise at most 0.5 per unit of run).

## Hard rules (the harness will reject you otherwise)

- Every pattern must have at least one socket, and sockets must sit exactly on the footprint edge at
  walking height, with a width of at least 3 u.
- Platforms are axis-aligned boxes. Use `RAMP_PX`, `RAMP_NX`, `RAMP_PZ`, `RAMP_NZ` for slopes; a ramp's
  `min.y` is its low-edge surface and `max.y` its high-edge surface, and its rise over run must not
  exceed 0.5. NO stairs anywhere: never stack three or more platforms rising in equal small steps.
- Gift slots sit on NORMAL platform tops only, at `top + 0.3`, at least 2.0 u apart and 0.5 u from
  edges. Altar slots on NORMAL tops, 1.5 u from edges.
- Any two platforms that overlap in XZ must have at least 4.0 u of vertical clearance between the
  lower one's top and the upper one's bottom.
- Jumps inside a pattern must stay inside the design hop envelope: a plain jump climbs at most +2.0 u
  and crosses at most 6.0 u edge-to-edge at a flat rise (less as the rise grows); a jump pad reaches
  +3.5 u within 4.5 u. Landing platforms need at least 2.0 u of horizontal extent.
- No hard-coded level numbers anywhere: `min_level` is the only level gate.

## Output format

One block: `=== FILE: design/patterns-b.json ===` ... `=== END FILE ===`, containing valid JSON.
Do not emit anything else.

## Done when

The file parses as JSON, every pattern validates against the spec, and the harness in
`server/crates/sim/tests/patterns.rs` still passes with your file loaded. In NOTES list the pattern
count, the tag coverage, and any spec ambiguity you resolved yourself.
