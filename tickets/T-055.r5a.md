Target: INKLING (max effort)

## Context
The chunk assembler works (maps 65-280 platforms, valid per `levelgen::validate`). But the physics bot's failures
show route edges that are technically inside the hop envelope yet unfair for humans too, e.g. (count, gap, rise,
from size -> to size, kinds):
@include tickets/T-055.r5.edges.txt
Jumping 6-7 u onto a 1 u wide ledge is pixel-perfect. The human wants fun, fair big maps.

## Task
1. `server/crates/sim/src/levelgen.rs` (FN/APPEND edits): tighten the jump rules used by the platform graph
   (`can_jump_or_step` / `design_max_gap` / `platform_graph`, and therefore `validate` and the bot):
   - A JUMP edge (not a touching walk/step/drop) needs a landing platform whose smaller horizontal extent is
     >= 2.0 u (jump pads excepted as landing targets only if >= 2.0 u too).
   - Comfort margin: a jump's edge-to-edge gap must be <= (design max gap for its rise) - 1.0 u.
   - Touching drops (gap ~0, rise < -0.55) are always fine (walk off the edge).
   Keep generation succeeding: if a chunk becomes internally unreachable under the new rules, fix the chunk data
   (task 2) rather than loosening the rules. Keep the levelgen tests passing (update numbers in tests only where
   these rules change them, and say so).
2. `design/chunks.json` (FILE, keep one chunk per line): fix every chunk whose internal jumps now violate the rules
   (widen narrow landing ledges to >= 2 u, shorten gaps, add a step), keeping the chunk's character and its gift
   slots valid.
3. `tools/validate-chunks.mjs` (FILE): apply the same two jump rules in its reachability graph, and add an epsilon
   of 1e-6 to threshold comparisons.
After your change: `node tools/validate-chunks.mjs` passes, and generation stays valid for 30 seeds x levels 1..12
(the existing levelgen tests check this).
