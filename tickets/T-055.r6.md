# Ticket T-055 r6: replace the route bot with an EDGE-FEASIBILITY physics test
Target: INKLING (max effort)

## Context
`levelgen::validate` proves every gift/altar/beacon is connected over the platform graph (`levelgen::platform_graph`)
whose edges are: touching walk/step (rise <= 0.55 u), touching drops, jumps (landing >= 2 u wide, gap <= design
envelope - 1 u), jump-pad launches. The full-route physics bot in `server/crates/sim/tests/playthrough.rs` still fails
56/60 levels after five rounds - route-following over huge maps is the fragile part, not the maps. What we actually
need to prove with real physics is that EVERY KIND OF EDGE the graph admits is doable with the real controller.

## Task (output the complete playthrough.rs as FILE)
1. New test `every_graph_edge_kind_is_physically_doable`: for 10 seeds x levels 1..10 (`levelgen::generate`), walk
   every edge of `platform_graph` and classify it as walk/step-up/drop/jump/pad. For JUMP and PAD edges, bucket by
   (gap rounded up to 0.25 u, rise rounded to 0.25 u, landing smaller extent rounded down to 0.5 u, launcher is pad).
   For each bucket build a tiny two-platform world: platform A (12 u long run-up along the jump direction, 4 u
   wide, top at 0) and B (landing extent = bucket width, depth = 3 u, top at bucket rise, edge-to-edge gap = bucket
   gap; for pads A is a 3x3 PLATFORM_JUMP_PAD). Simulate the REAL controller (`controller::step`): run at full input
   from the far end of A straight at B's centre, jump on the last frame still grounded on A (coyote allowed), hold
   jump for the full rise, steer to B's centre while airborne; success = grounded on B's top within 3 s.
   Also simulate one representative step-up (0.5 u), stair (4 risers of 0.5 u x 1 u treads) and drop (-6 u) case.
   The test passes when every bucket succeeds; print a table of buckets (count, gap, rise, width, pad, ok) and, on
   failure, the failing buckets with the closest landing distance.
2. Keep the hand-authored-level full playthrough test as it is (it passes). Keep the generated-levels route bot as
   `#[ignore]` diagnostic (`cargo test -- --ignored` to run), unchanged otherwise.
3. If a bucket is genuinely impossible, do NOT loosen the test: list it in NOTES (gap/rise/width) - the lead will
   tighten the map rules.
