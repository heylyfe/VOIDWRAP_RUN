# Ticket T-068b: ramp content, chunk scale, and ramp visuals

Runs in parallel with T-068 (which implements the slope collision in the shared `sim` crate). You touch
ONLY `design/` and `web/src/scene/`. Never touch `server/`.

## The ramp model (frozen, same as T-068)

Four platform kinds now exist, generated into `web/src/protocol/generated.ts`:
`PLATFORM_RAMP_PX = 3`, `PLATFORM_RAMP_NX = 4`, `PLATFORM_RAMP_PZ = 5`, `PLATFORM_RAMP_NZ = 6`
(alongside `PLATFORM_NORMAL = 0`, `PLATFORM_CRUMBLE = 1`, `PLATFORM_JUMP_PAD = 2`).

For a ramp platform:
- `bounds.min.y` is the surface height at the LOW edge.
- `bounds.max.y` is the surface height at the HIGH edge.
- The surface is a straight line between them across the slope axis:
  `RAMP_PX` low at min.x high at max.x; `RAMP_NX` low at max.x high at min.x;
  `RAMP_PZ` low at min.z high at max.z; `RAMP_NZ` low at max.z high at min.z.
- The solid volume is everything below that surface.

## Part 1 - chunk format spec (`design/CHUNK_FORMAT.md`)

- Add the four ramp kinds to the `platforms` kind list.
- Replace the "Stair steps: risers of 0.5 u ... treads >= 1.0 u deep" rule with a ramp rule:
  a ramp's rise over its run must not exceed 0.5 (about 26.6 degrees), its run must be at least 2.0 u,
  and its width across the direction of travel must be at least 3 u. State that stairs no longer exist
  anywhere in the game.
- Keep every other rule intact (sockets, gift slots, stacking clearance, hop envelope).

## Part 2 - chunk catalog (`design/chunks.json`)

- Convert `ch05_stair_of_embers` into a ramp chunk: one `RAMP_PZ` platform from y=0 at z=0 to y=6 at
  z=15, 14 u wide, replacing the 14 one-unit steps. Keep the id, name, role, sockets, gift slots and
  tags, but replace the `stairs` tag with `ramp` and keep `climb` and `embers`. Move the gift slots
  onto the ramp surface (their y must equal the ramp surface height at their x/z plus 0.3).
- Audit ALL 25 chunks for stair-like sequences (three or more stacked platforms whose tops rise in
  equal small increments along one axis) and convert each to a single ramp of the same footprint and
  the same total rise. If a sequence is really a terrace (two or three large platforms with big
  treads), leave it alone - terraces are not stairs.
- Scale check against the reference: platforms are 10-20 u across, path height differences are 1-6 u,
  and the player is 1.8 u tall. Adjust any chunk that is far outside that band (tiny 2-3 u platforms,
  or height jumps over 6 u in one move). Do not redesign chunks that already fit.
- Every chunk must still satisfy the existing rules: sockets on the footprint edge at walking height,
  gift slots on NORMAL tops only, >= 2.0 u apart, >= 0.5 u from edges, stacking clearance >= 4.0 u.

## Part 3 - ramp visuals (`web/src/scene/Platforms.tsx`, `web/src/scene/platformUtils.ts`)

- Ramps must render as solid wedges, not boxes: the top face follows the slope, the low end has no
  thickness, the high end is `max.y - min.y` tall.
- Keep the existing instanced rendering approach. The clean way is a second `InstancedMesh` with a
  unit wedge geometry (a box whose top face is sloped from 0 at one edge to 1 at the other), scaled
  per instance by the platform's size and rotated per ramp kind. Do not switch the whole scene to
  per-platform meshes.
- Ramps must keep the existing surface material, palette, ice tint, crumble shake and gone-sink
  behaviour. A crumble ramp must still visibly shake and sink.
- `platformUtils.ts`: add a helper that reports whether a kind is a ramp and the slope axis, so the
  renderer and any decor placement agree. Keep `isPillar`, `platformCenter`, `platformSize` working.
- Decor placement (`web/src/scene/decorPlacement.ts`) must not place decor floating over a ramp's
  low end: use the ramp surface height, not `max.y`, when deciding where decor sits.

## Part 4 - stair audit

- Grep the whole repo for `stair` (case-insensitive) and remove or rework every reference: content
  tags, chunk ids/names, comments that describe stairs as a live mechanic, and any test that asserts
  stair behaviour. Historical notes in `VERIFICATION_LOG.md` and old tickets stay as they are.
- `node tools/validate-content.mjs` must stay green.
