# Ticket T-084: greater curses + the stair-reference audit

A tier above normal curses: run-shaping threats with escape mechanics. At least three, anchored by
Wreath. They enter through pedestal voting and are clearly marked as greater.

## What exists (verified)

- `design/content.json` has a `curses` section; `ModCard` already carries a `greater` flag.
- `server/crates/server/src/run.rs` applies curses as mods; `server/crates/sim/src/rules.rs` holds the
  enemy brains.
- T-068 removed stairs from the game: ramps replaced them everywhere.

## Part 1 - greater curses (at least three)

1. **Wreath** (the anchor). Lands on a player's head and cries. If it cries too long, that player dies.
   Remove it with a jump pad or a glider draft - the level's own geometry is the counter. It must be
   visible on the victim and audible/visible to teammates so they can help.
2. **Tithe**. Every gift collected costs a small amount of the run's currency; at zero currency the
   player is slowed until they collect again.
3. **Hush**. The HUD's gift direction hint is disabled and fog thickens; the counter is the radar
   module from T-086 or a teammate's callout.

Each needs: a clear telegraph, a real escape mechanic that uses existing geometry or abilities, and a
test proving the escape works and the failure state kills.

## Part 2 - the stair-reference audit

Stairs no longer exist. Audit the whole repo for anything that spawns, references or assumes them:
content tags, chunk ids and names, comments describing stairs as a live mechanic, tests asserting
stair behaviour, and any generator rule that produces a stair sequence. Rework or remove each one.
Historical notes in `VERIFICATION_LOG.md` and old tickets stay as they are.

Document the audit in NOTES: every hit, and what you did with it.

## Output format

Complete files with `=== FILE: <path> ===` ... `=== END FILE ===`, or single functions with
`=== FN: <path> :: <name> ===` ... `=== END FN ===`. Allowed paths: `server/crates/server/src/`,
`server/crates/sim/src/`, `design/content.json`, `design/chunks.json`.

Do not touch `web/` or `protocol/` unless a client-visible field is genuinely required; if it is,
change `protocol/schema.json` and note that the lead must run `just gen` and `just fixtures`.

## Done when

`cargo test -p server` and `cargo test -p sim` pass, `node tools/validate-content.mjs` passes, clippy is
clean, and the audit is documented. Put the numbers you chose in NOTES.
