# Ticket T-055 r3: the bot's walk-edge steering targets a point under its own feet
Target: INKLING (max effort)

Your r2 bot was applied (playthrough.rs + the appended `platform_graph` in levelgen.rs). It fails 59/60 levels.
Almost every failure is the same: on platform 1 (the spawn hub's beacon dais, a 3x3 u box 0.5 u high RESTING ON the
hub floor, platform 0) with "next platform 0, edge Walk": the bot sits at (8.15, 0.5, 8.15) forever. Diagnosis by
the lead: for a Walk edge where the target platform's top footprint CONTAINS the current platform's footprint (a dais
on a floor, a stair step on a landing), "the closest point on the shared edge" is directly under the bot, so it never
moves. Fix the steering: when stepping DOWN onto a platform that lies under the current one, steer to the nearest
point of the target top that is OUTSIDE the current platform's footprint by at least player half-width + 0.3 u
(prefer the side facing the following waypoint); same idea for stepping UP onto a platform that sits on the current
one. Also look at the Jump-edge failures (e.g. "on platform 22, next 27, edge Jump") - check the takeoff point and the
hop commitment for jumps between platforms at different heights.
