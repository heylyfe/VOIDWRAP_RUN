# Ticket T-076 (retry 1): the eleven new enemies have no client model

## Why this is a retry

The first attempt produced `web/src/scene/enemyVisuals.ts` — a pure data module describing
silhouettes and telegraphs — plus its test. Nothing imports it. It is dead code and it does not
change a single pixel. The real gap is much simpler and much bigger:

**The client renders nine enemy models. The server now spawns twenty enemies.**

`EnemyView.kind` is the content kind id, i.e. the index into `design/content.json`'s `enemies`
array. That array grew from 9 to 20 in T-072. `web/src/scene/enemyLook.ts` still has nine
`ENEMY_LOOKS` entries and `web/src/scene/EnemyField.tsx` still has nine `MODEL_COMPONENTS`.
`enemyLookFor(kind)` falls back to the pallbearer look for any kind >= 9, so **Rewind, Warden,
Peal, Kite, Static, Blight, Metronome, Overlook, Chorus, Coil and Lure all render as
Pallbearers today.** That is the bug this ticket fixes.

## What you must deliver

1. **Extend the roster to twenty.** `ENEMY_LOOKS` in `web/src/scene/enemyLook.ts` gains eleven
   entries, in `design/content.json` order, appended after `corrupter`:
   `rewind, warden, peal, kite, static, blight, metronome, overlook, chorus, coil, lure`.
   `EnemyBehaviorId` gains the same eleven members. `ENEMY_KIND_COUNT` becomes 20 automatically.
   Give each one an `accent` and a `glow` colour that is distinct from the other nineteen and
   consistent with `design/VISUAL_DIRECTION.md` (danger reads red; the Lure keeps its purple-fog
   identity; Static is fog-grey; Blight is sickly green; Metronome is a warm brass beat).

2. **Eleven new model components** in `web/src/scene/enemies/`, one file each, following the
   existing pattern exactly (`Pallbearer.tsx` is the reference: `"use client"`, `EnemyModelProps`,
   a `useFrame` that reads `anim.current`, unit-radius geometry, the parent scales by body radius,
   `ENEMY_DORMANT` dims, `ENEMY_TELEGRAPH`/`ENEMY_ATTACK` drive the wind-up). Register them in
   `MODEL_COMPONENTS` in `EnemyField.tsx` in the same order.

   Each silhouette must be readable at gameplay distance and must not be a recoloured Pallbearer.
   The registry contract for each is inlined below — build the shape from it:
   - **Rewind** — a thin replay ghost: a translucent body trailing a short ribbon of its own past
     positions, trembling during the telegraph.
   - **Warden** — a squat post with a wide flat ring at its base (the guard ring is the model).
   - **Peal** — a bell: a tapered shell that visibly opens at the lip during the telegraph.
   - **Kite** — a tall thin spindle with a taut tether line running from its tip.
   - **Static** — a low fog-grey lump that is nearly invisible; the fog patch is the read.
   - **Blight** — a wet, lumpy mass with a spreading discoloured skirt.
   - **Metronome** — an upright arm on a plinth that swings; the arm flashes before the beat.
   - **Overlook** — a perched eye on a stalk, high off the ground, with a glowing mark.
   - **Chorus** — a cluster of small pipes/organ bodies that murmur.
   - **Coil** — a wound spring that visibly tightens (scale down + spin up) as it winds.
   - **Lure** — the owner's star-shaped fog creature: a flat five-point star silhouette sitting in
     a purple fog pool, pulsing slowly, reading as a gift at distance. Keep it flat and low.

3. **Telegraphs for the new enemies** in `web/src/scene/Telegraphs.tsx`. The existing `switch
   (look.behavior)` at line ~144 handles the nine old ones. Add arms for the new ones that have a
   floor cue: Rewind (shrinking return mark), Warden (ring around the guarded gift), Peal
   (expanding ring), Kite (tether line + strike marker), Overlook (sight line), Coil (expanding
   shockwave ring), Metronome (the swing lane). Static, Blight, Chorus and Lure are tier 3 /
   ambient — they get no decal; say so in a comment rather than leaving a silent fallthrough.

4. **Delete `web/src/scene/enemyVisuals.ts` and `web/src/scene/enemyVisuals.test.ts`.** They are
   unused. Do not keep them "for reference".

5. **Tests.** Extend `web/src/scene/enemyLook.test.ts`: twenty looks, twenty distinct behaviours,
   twenty distinct accents, `enemyLookFor(19)` is the lure and not the pallbearer,
   `enemyLookFor(20)` still falls back safely, and `MODEL_COMPONENTS.length === ENEMY_KIND_COUNT`
   (export the array or a length from `EnemyField.tsx` if that is the cleanest way — a plain
   `export const MODEL_COMPONENTS` is fine).
