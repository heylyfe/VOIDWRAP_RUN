## FROZEN CONTRACT (implement exactly; no agent may change it)

- `GET /health` → `200`, empty body.
- `POST /matches` body `{"name", "version", "port", "players", "max_players",
  "level", "phase", "joinable", "address": str|null}` →
  `200 {"id", "secret", "address"}`. Address: use `address` verbatim when
  supplied, otherwise the request source IP (trust `X-Forwarded-For` — Render
  fronts services with a proxy — falling back to the socket peer). `id`/
  `secret` random, secret unguessable. `level`/`phase` opaque strings — store
  and echo.
- `POST /matches/{id}` body `{"secret", "players", "level", "phase", "joinable"}` →
  `200`; `404` expired; `403` wrong secret.
- `DELETE /matches/{id}` body `{"secret"}` → `200`; `403`; `404`.
- `GET /matches?version=<str>` → `200` array of `{"name", "address",
  "players", "max_players", "level", "phase", "joinable", "version"}`,
  exact-match version filter, `last_seen` newer than 30 s only, response
  carries `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. Entries expire 30 s after last
  heartbeat; sweep every 10 s. The secret is the only authority — no accounts.
