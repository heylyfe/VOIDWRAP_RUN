# v0.25.0-alpha.1 — Refinement Cycle 13

Three audits that were passing for the wrong reason, and one measurement that had
been missing since T-115.

## Gifts can no longer spawn inside anything

The rule was already "never inside a platform", but the audit measured it with its
own copy of the box test that had no epsilon margin — so a gift touching a platform
face by less than a millimetre counted as a defect. Five "hits" out of 755,878 were
measurement error, and the threshold had been loosened to 0.01 % to absorb them.

The audit now calls the generator's own predicate, and the threshold is **zero**.
Measured: **0 intersecting gifts out of 755,816**.

## The reference scale now explains itself

Six patterns owned all 8,081 out-of-band placements while the aggregate rate passed
at 3.85 %. Three of them had no excuse and were fixed (`ch25_hanging_gallery`
21 -> 20 u, `ch09_jump_pad_lift` and `ch16_misers_loft` 8 -> 10 u). The other three
are **ramp-forced**: a chunk that drops 5 u needs 10 u of ramp at the 0.5 slope cap,
so its footprint cannot be small. Those are documented rather than reshaped.

The metric now names each offender with its extent, its drop, and the footprint its
drop forces. **3.85 % -> 2.11 %**, and every remaining outlier is explained.

## More trampolines and tripmines

Three patterns were carrying every trampoline in the game. Five new patterns, all
additive: three trampoline (`pa2_ember_bounce`, `pb_frost_bounce`,
`pb_void_bounce`) and two tripmine (`pa2_mine_gauntlet`, `pa3_mine_switchback`).
Catalog 135 -> **140**. Trampoline patterns 3 -> 6, tripmine 4 -> 6.

One of them was silently ineligible — `mech:fork` is only legal on a junction, and
the piece is a two-socket path, so it placed zero times over 6,000 levels. Fixed.

## Death causes are now visible

The playthrough kit could see that its bots died but not where or why. A new
optional NDJSON death log (`VOIDWRAP_DEATH_LOG`) records position, velocity, cause
and remaining lives for every death, and the gate prints a summary line on every
run:

```
PASS - death-log (8 deaths (void:8), 8 ended a run, deepest fall y=-17.21; levels: 1)
```

The first measurement: **every death is a fall, and every one ends the run.** Not
one enemy kill. `Run::new` starts with 0 lives and the only spare is the rare
`spare_wick` upgrade, so the first missed jump of a run is always fatal. That is a
balance decision and is left for the owner's balancing pass — it is now measurable.
