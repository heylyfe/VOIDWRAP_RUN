**Pre-release alpha** - the third playable build of VOIDWRAP RUN. Expect bugs and big changes.

## How to play
Windows gets an installer, and it is now the recommended way to play: download the file ending in `...-windows-setup.exe` from this release, double-click it, click "More info" and "Run anyway" if Windows asks (it does this the first time, for every new game from a small developer), then "Next" -> "Install" -> "Finish". That is all - the game gets its own icon and a Start menu entry. No administrator rights needed. Uninstall any time through Windows Settings > Apps or from the Start menu.

Prefer the old way? The zip download still works: **extract it** (right-click -> "Extract All..."), then double-click **VOIDWRAP RUN**. If the window stays empty or white, double-click **PLAY-IN-BROWSER** instead. Step-by-step help for every click: see `HOW-TO-PLAY.md` inside the download or [in the repository](https://github.com/heylyfe/VOIDWRAP_RUN/blob/main/HOW-TO-PLAY.md).

## What's new in this alpha
- **A Windows installer** (recommended): installs only for your user, adds the game to the Start menu with its own icon, and can add a desktop shortcut. The zip download still works exactly as before.
- **Much bigger maps**, built from hand-made pieces the game puts together: walkway layers stacked on pillars, stairs you can walk up without jumping, tall towers, bridges with a second road on top, glowing jump pads that throw you about 4 metres into the air, and paths that loop and split. Every map is checked so every gift and the beacon can be reached.
- **Two-phase levels**: collect every small gift (about 50 on level 1, more each level, up to 400), then a 3-second warning sounds and the map **collapses from the outside in** - tiles shake and flash before they fall, and the spawn platform never falls. Every spot you emptied becomes a **golden gift** (1 gold each, shared team money) to grab while you run for the **beacon** on the spawn platform. Golden gifts on fallen tiles are lost.
- **A new class: Bulwark** replaces the Blinker. On the ground, root yourself in place for 1.6 seconds (Shift or the right mouse button): you cannot move, but a pulse pushes everything nearby away and almost nothing can hurt you - falling shells still can. Long 16-second cooldown. The other classes stay the same: Striker (dash), Grappler (auto-aimed grapple), Glider (extra mid-air jump and gliding).
- **All nine hunters reworked**: each now tests its own skill, every deadly attack is announced by a visible warning (a ring, a line or a flare) 0.6 to 2.5 seconds before it lands, and some hunters push or slow you instead of killing. The full hunter guide, `ENEMIES.md`, is in the game folder.
- **Mouse lock**: press **Ctrl** to lock or free the mouse - the game shows the key at the bottom of the screen. Clicking the game also locks it.
- **New shop prices**, about double the old ones - levels now pay out far more gold.

## What earlier alphas already had
- Lobby on a shared spawn platform: vote on which **enemies hunt you** and pick an **upgrade or curse**
- A lobby shop, **altars** (one random upgrade and one random curse each) and the level events **High Rise**, **Slick Ice** and **Crumbling**
- **Lives**, and finished or out players become invisible flying spectators
- **Play with up to 8 friends** from your own computer - on your Wi-Fi, or anywhere else
- Running, jumping, falling, landing and dashing animations, and per-level visual themes with their own screen filter
