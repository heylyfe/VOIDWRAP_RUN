**Pre-release alpha** - the first playable build of VOIDWRAP RUN. Expect bugs and big changes.

## How to play
Download the file for your system below (Windows: `...-windows.zip`), **extract it** (right-click -> "Extract All..."),
then double-click **PLAY**. Step-by-step help for every click: see `HOW-TO-PLAY.md` inside the download or
[in the repository](https://github.com/heylyfe/VOIDWRAP_RUN/blob/main/HOW-TO-PLAY.md).

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

## What's in this alpha
- Lobby on a shared spawn platform: vote on which **enemies hunt you** and pick an **upgrade or curse** (cards explain everything)
- **Randomly generated levels** of checkerboard roads in a coloured void - a new map every level, every map tested to be completable
- Collect the gifts -> **golden gifts** (money for later) appear and the **beacon** opens -> back to the lobby for the next pick
- 6 enemies, 10 upgrades, 13 curses (greater curses every 10th level), extra lives
- **Multiplayer on the same network** (up to 8): `PLAY-WITH-FRIENDS`; finished or out players become invisible flying spectators
- Running, jumping, falling, landing and dashing animations; four visual themes with their own screen filter
