# v0.29.0-alpha.1 — Refinement Cycle 17

Voting, the shop and altars finally have end-to-end coverage.

## The gap this closes

Cycle 16 established that the automated playthrough cannot reach level 2, and that the
cause is the starting-lives balance decision plus the bot's hold-still fallback. Voting,
the shop and altars all sit behind level 2, so **three shipped systems had no
end-to-end coverage at all**.

The debug command layer can drive them directly, in singleplayer, without needing a bot
to survive a level. This release adds the kit that does it.

## `tools/debug-conformance/run.mjs` — 13 checks, 13 passing

It speaks the real wire format through the voting kit's `wire.mjs`, so a protocol
change breaks it the same way it breaks the voting kit. `wire.mjs` gained the two
variants it was missing: `ClientMsg::Debug` and `ServerMsg::DebugResult`.

## The bug it found

`debug_switch_level` never refreshed the shop. `make_shop` returns `None` at level 1 and
was only ever called from `finish_level`, so **a tester who jumped to level 10 still saw
no shop at all** — the entire shop was unreachable from the debug layer, which is
exactly the system the layer exists to make testable.

The shop is priced by level bracket, so jumping levels has to re-price it. One line in
`run.rs`. After the fix the kit sees a `Shop` frame with 11 items on join at level 6.

This is the project's recurring defect class again — "the piece exists but never reaches
the player" — this time in the debug layer rather than in level generation.

## Tuning notes for the later balancing pass

- **Starting lives = 0** remains the single largest obstacle to automated coverage. It
  is why the playthrough cannot reach level 2, and why voting has no eight-ballot test.
- Voting still has no end-to-end test with eight real ballots. The next step is to let
  `debug_switch_level` open a vote session directly, which would close that gap without
  touching the balance decision.
