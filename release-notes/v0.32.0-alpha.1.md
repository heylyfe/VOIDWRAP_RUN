# v0.32.0-alpha.1 — Refinement Cycle 20

Colour-identity cycle, aimed at the owner's directive #4 ("make the models look good")
in the one part of it that can be measured: **at gameplay distance the only thing
separating one enemy from another is its accent colour**, and the only thing separating a
real gift from LURE is its glow.

## T-145 — enemy accents are perceptually distinct

Measured in CIELAB, four pairs of enemy accents were indistinguishable and one enemy
vanished into a theme's fog:

| pair | delta-E |
|---|---|
| `pallbearer` `#ff3355` vs `peal` `#e63946` | 10.5 |
| `gale_wight` `#d94fff` vs `mimic` `#ff3df0` | 17.4 |
| `snapshrike` `#ff4436` vs `peal` `#e63946` | 17.6 |
| `pallbearer` `#ff3355` vs `snapshrike` `#ff4436` | 19.7 |
| `static` `#9aa3ad` vs **ashfall fog** `#6d6873` | 22.5 |

RGB distance hid this: `#ff3355` and `#e63946` differ by 0x0D in red but only 10.5 in
CIELAB. New `enemyPalette.ts` does sRGB → CIELAB and CIE76 delta-E with no dependency.

Four accents moved, identities preserved: `peal` → oxblood `#9d0208`, `gale_wight` → deep
violet `#7b2ff7`, `static` → cold steel `#a8b8c8`, `pallbearer` → deeper rose `#e91e63`.
**Closest pair 10.5 → 22.8, worst fog contrast 22.5 → 30.3.**

Because the accent is also the telegraph colour, this improves telegraph legibility too.

### The table was not the source of truth

The accents were duplicated between `ENEMY_LOOKS` and each model's hardcoded `emissive`
literal, so a palette change could land in the table and never reach the screen. Two
guards now read the model sources: every model must light itself with its own accent or
glow, and no model may render another enemy's accent. Writing them surfaced that
`RiftWarden` and `BoltSentry` legitimately light themselves with their glow.

## T-146 — the straggler beam wore LURE's colour

JUDGMENT CALL 2 required the straggler highlight to be clearly different from the normal
glow, golden gifts, and LURE's glimmer. It was not:

| pair | delta-E |
|---|---|
| normal gift glow `#dfe6ff` vs straggler beam `#dfe6ff` | **0.0** |
| normal gift glow `#dfe6ff` vs LURE shimmer `#dfe6ff` | **0.0** |

`BEAM_COLOR` was byte-identical to `COMMON.gift.glow`, which is exactly what `Lure.tsx`
wears for its disguise — so the marker whose job is to say "this one is real" wore the
fake's colour. The original comment reasoned that "a third colour would collide with
both"; that is only true of a badly chosen third colour. `#7cff4f` sits **43.1** delta-E
from the nearest colour in the game.

Gift identity colours moved to a pure `giftPalette.ts` so they can be measured, and
`giftPalette.test.ts` asserts the beam is ≥ 30 delta-E from the normal glow (and
therefore LURE), the golden gift, the beacon, and all 20 enemy accents.

## Tuning notes

- The four new enemy accents are all darker or cooler than what they replaced. Measured
  fog contrast went **up**, so this is a net gain, but if any enemy now reads too dim in
  play the lever is lightness, not the delta-E floor.
- The straggler beam is now a saturated lime rather than a pale blue. If it proves too
  loud, darken `BEAM_COLOR` (a `#4fbf2f` keeps the hue separation) rather than lowering
  `BEAM_MIN_DELTA_E` — the floor is what makes the marker trustworthy.
