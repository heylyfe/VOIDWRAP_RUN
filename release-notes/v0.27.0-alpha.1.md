# v0.27.0-alpha.1 — Refinement Cycle 15

Two measurement tickets, both of which turned out to be the same shape: a number that
had been accepted without asking where it came from.

## The death log can now tell an edge fall from a seam fall

An **edge fall** is a navigation problem — the player walked off. A **seam fall** is a
geometry problem — a platform was right there and the collision layer missed it. They
need different fixes, and the log could not tell them apart.

The first attempt measured the distance from the death position to the nearest
platform. That is worthless: a 16 u fall takes about a second, and a player moving
6 u/s drifts 6 u sideways in that time, so every death reported drift as distance.

The log now measures from the **last position where the player had ground contact**,
and reports `ground_dist` (distance to the nearest platform footprint) and
`ground_inset` (how far inside it).

**Result: 7 deaths, every inset negative and small (−0.21 to −0.48). Zero seam
falls.** Each player left from just past the lip, inside the coyote window. The
collision layer is not dropping anyone through geometry, which agrees with the sim's
route tests that walk the real controller across every platform seam.

## `5.heights` fixed at the data, threshold back to 0

This was the last audit metric whose threshold had been raised to fit the data (2% to
5% by owner decision) rather than the data fixed. It sat at 3.59%.

The metric now names which patterns own the out-of-band deltas and whether they are
too small or too large. That showed **all 15,352 violations came from one chunk**,
`ch02_hollow_market`, and **all were too small**: four rim strips sitting 0.5 u above
the bowl floor, a half-step between the `y=0` ring and the `y=-1` floor. Two
half-steps per strip, four strips, 3,838 placements.

Raising the strips to `y=0` makes the bowl a clean 1 u pit. **15,352 → 0**, rate
**3.59% → 0.00%**, and the threshold goes **5% → 0** so a half-step anywhere fails
immediately. Negative-tested: reverting one strip gives 3,838 violations and a FAIL
naming the chunk.
