**Pre-release alpha** — the multiplayer lobby browser. Expect bugs and changes.

## How to play
Same as before. Windows: download the file ending in `...-windows-setup.exe` from this release and double-click it (click "More info" and "Run anyway" if Windows asks), or use the zip: **extract it**, then double-click **VOIDWRAP RUN**. If the window stays empty or white, double-click **PLAY-IN-BROWSER**. Step-by-step help: `HOW-TO-PLAY.md` inside the download or [in the repository](https://github.com/heylyfe/VOIDWRAP_RUN/blob/main/HOW-TO-PLAY.md).

## What's new in this alpha
- **Open lobbies.** The join panel now lists games other people are hosting right now: name, address, how many players are in, the level and what the lobby is doing. Click one and you join it, exactly as if you had pasted the address yourself.
- **Hosts announce themselves.** When you turn hosting on, the game registers with a small matchmaker service and keeps it up to date every 10 seconds with your player count, level and phase. Turn hosting off and the listing disappears immediately; if your game crashes, the listing expires on its own within about 40 seconds.
- **Only reachable games are joinable.** If your router could not open a port, your game is still listed but greyed out with "host is unreachable from the internet", so nobody wastes a click.
- **Long hosting sessions keep working.** The router's port mapping used to lapse after two hours; it is now renewed every 90 minutes.
- **The list only shows your version.** You see games running the same build as you, so a mismatch cannot half-connect.
- **Cold starts are handled.** The free hosting tier sleeps when idle; the list shows "Waking server…" while it comes back instead of an error.

## Under the hood
- New `matchmaker` service (Rust, axum) deployed alongside the game on Render, with a frozen HTTP contract and a black-box conformance kit that proves it.
- The game server talks to it directly, never your browser, so a friend's machine is never asked to reach anything.

## Not yet
Accounts, cosmetics, sound, more classes and events. Private lobbies and friend invites are still manual: share your address.
